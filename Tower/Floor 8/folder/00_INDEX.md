## What's new in this revision

- **Split into 16 focused files** instead of one long document.
- **Eight NPC archetype tables** (`06`) replacing the old single generic "Common House NPC" table — each with Flavor, Trade, Clue, Favor, and Quest-hook results.
- **Every NPC archetype has a balanced GP value** (`07`) — small, formula-based, and explicitly kept a minority slice of the Pool so Gathering, Crafting, and Robbery remain the main ways to make money.
- **Guard patrols now respond in real time**, not just the next morning (`11`).
- **The Syndicate now guarantees at least some malice every day** once Pressure is Active or higher, instead of relying purely on the interruption die (`13`).
- **Guild Hall quests are tiered and pay better** (`09`) — Minor / Standard / Major, with Major quests feeding into the hidden storyline.
- **A hidden, clue-gated storyline** (`14`) — "The Ledger's Debt" — that lets one team take down the Iron Ledger's financial network directly and walk away with a guaranteed 1st-place lead, funded by a *separate* recovered-gold reserve that does **not** draw from the 3,000gp town Pool.

## The economy at a glance

- **Town Economy Pool: 3,000gp hard cap.** Every gp a player earns from selling, quests, alms, or graveyards comes out of this pool. It never refills. See `01` for full tiering.
- **NPC interaction gold is a small, separate slice (~150–180gp) of that same Pool** — see `07`.
- **Storyline Reward Reserve: 275gp, entirely outside the Pool.** This is Iron Ledger embezzlement money recovered by the team that finishes the hidden storyline — it doesn't drain the town, so it doesn't trigger recession tiers. See `14`.

Everything else — rules text, DCs, tables — is preserved from the original design; nothing mechanical was quietly dropped, only reorganized and expanded where you asked for more.
