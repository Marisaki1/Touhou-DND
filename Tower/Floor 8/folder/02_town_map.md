# FLOOR 8: THE MERCHANT'S CROSSING
### Part 2 — Town Map & Districts

The town is laid out around the Market Square, with the Gathering Areas reached by carriage to the west and the Slums lying further out along the same road, east of town.

## District Table

| Zone | Location | Contains |
|---|---|---|
| **Market Square** | Center | The four player stalls, the fountain, Soren's booth |
| **Church** | North | Religion checks for alms, entry point to the Pristine Graveyard |
| **Pristine Graveyard** | North, behind the Church | One-time high-value heist; requires sneaking through the Church |
| **Common Graveyard** | North, adjacent | Public salvage — no sneaking required |
| **Common Houses** | East | 22 Small, 14 Medium, 6 Large — residential lanes, robbery targets, NPC interactions |
| **Crafting Houses** | South | Where all Craft Major Actions happen; a handful of recurring NPC crafters |
| **Merchant District** | South, adjacent Crafting Houses | Warehouses, bulk suppliers, the Grey Ledger (from Day 3) |
| **Guild House** | Northeast, extremely large | Quest board, guard origin point, safe zone |
| **Syndicate House** | South, extremely large | The Iron Ledger's public-facing headquarters — imposing, heavily deterred, not enterable without serious investigation. **Distinct from the Underground** (see below), which is the hidden tunnel network beneath the Merchant District. |
| **The Underground** | Hidden, beneath the Merchant District | Mining Area access, Syndicate investigation, the Iron Ledger Charter, and the entry point to the hidden storyline (`14`) |
| **Free Spaces (×12)** | Scattered, 2–3 per district | DM-flex plots — Daily Events, quest hooks, flavor. See table below. |
| **Carriage Road** | West | Leads to the four Gathering Areas |
| **The Road (east)** | Beyond town | Leads to the Slums; shared with the Carriage Road as one continuous route, with town in the middle |

**Suggested distribution of the 12 Free Spaces:** 3 in Common Houses, 2 in the Merchant District, 2 near the Guild House, 2 near the Church/Graveyards, 2 along the Carriage Road, 1 in the Market Square itself. Adjust to taste — the table below works anywhere.

## Free Space Flavor Table — d12

Roll once per Free Space to seed it, or leave blank and use as-needed.

| d12 | Seed |
|---|---|
| 1 | Abandoned market stall — could be claimed/repaired for a Build/Expand-style bonus |
| 2 | Overgrown garden — minor one-time Forage flavor pickup, not a full Node |
| 3 | Town noticeboard — where flyers and rumors are posted |
| 4 | Old well — mostly flavor, a good quiet meeting spot |
| 5 | Burned-out shop husk — unsettling; possible Syndicate-arson clue |
| 6 | Kids playing — potential lead for the Cat Herder Guild quest, or a Young Boy/Young Girl encounter (`06`) |
| 7 | A busker's favorite corner — Performance-friendly |
| 8 | Second broken fountain — echoes the Market Square's, quiet |
| 9 | Guard's rest post — a friendly guard NPC willing to chat, good source of patrol-schedule info (`11`) |
| 10 | Boarded-up windows — rumor says a family left after "trouble" with the Iron Ledger; a soft hidden-storyline hook (`14`) |
| 11 | Stray cat colony — directly usable for the Cat Herder quest |
| 12 | Empty and forgettable — reserve for a future Daily Event |

**DM Note:** Free Spaces are also the easiest place to slot in an NPC interaction roll (`06`) when a player wanders somewhere with no fixed content — pick whichever archetype fits the seed above and roll their table.
