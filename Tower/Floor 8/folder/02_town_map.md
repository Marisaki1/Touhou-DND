# FLOOR 8: THE HUMAN VILLAGE
### Part 2 — Town Map & Districts

The village is laid out roughly west to east: the wild places where teams gather materials on one side, the Slums as the connecting stretch, and the village proper — Residential & Market, the Guild, and the Syndicate — on the other. Forest Forage sits apart, to the south, reached by its own path.

## District Table

| Zone | Position | Contains |
|---|---|---|
| **Wildlife Forest** | West (large) | Hunting Gathering Area — 20 nodes, see `03` |
| **Mining Cave** | West, below the Forest (large) | Mining Gathering Area — 20 nodes, see `03` |
| **The River Shallows** | Running along the west edge of the Slums | Fishing Gathering Area — 20 nodes, see `03` |
| **Slums** | Center, running north-south | The connecting district — Soup Kitchen, Fence, Fight Ring, Rumor Alley, see `10` |
| **The Old Path** | A thin strip east of the Slums | A quiet connecting road — flavor, Free Spaces, the occasional encounter, see `10` |
| **Residential & Market** | East (large) | The four player stalls, the fountain, the Masked White Girl's booth, the Small/Medium/Large houses, and the two graveyards on its outskirts — see `05`, `08` |
| **Guild** | East, beside Residential & Market | Quest board, guard origin point, safe zone, crafting — see `09` |
| **Syndicate** | South of Residential & Market | The Syndicate's public house — where teams go to pay, negotiate, or make trouble — see `13` |
| **Forest Forage** | South (large) | Foraging Gathering Area — 20 nodes, see `03` |

**Travel:** Wildlife Forest, Mining Cave, and the River Shallows are all reached by the same western road out of the Slums — the Shallows is simply the stretch of river the road runs alongside before it climbs toward the Forest and the Cave. Forest Forage is reached by its own southern path, past the Syndicate house. Any Travel action targeting a Gathering Area counts as travel through the Slums or past the Syndicate and risks an encounter — see `10`.

## Hop Count — Travel Times from the Stalls

Per Rule 11 (`01`), every hop between two directly connected places costs **2 RAP (10 minutes)**, flat. This table gives the hop count from Residential & Market (where the stalls are, and where a team starts every day) to everywhere else. For a trip between two non-hub places, route through Residential & Market unless the DM allows a narrative shortcut.

| Destination | Hops from Residential & Market | RAP Cost (round trip) |
|---|---|---|
| Guild | 1 | 4 RAP (20 min: 2 there, 2 back) |
| Syndicate | 1 | 4 RAP |
| Slums / The Old Path | 1 | 4 RAP |
| Wildlife Forest | 1 (via the Slums road) | 4 RAP |
| Mining Cave | 1 (via the Slums road) | 4 RAP |
| The River Shallows | 1 (via the Slums road) | 4 RAP |
| Forest Forage | 1 (via the Syndicate's southern path) | 4 RAP |

**Why every destination is a single hop:** the village is compact by design — no district is a cheaper or more expensive trip than any other, so the choice of where to gather or go is about the nodes and the risk (`03`, `13`), not travel-time arbitrage. Travel is Round Action time (RAP), separate from the 3 MAP (3-hour) cost of the Gather action itself (`03`, `04`) — a team pays the 4 RAP round trip on top of whichever gathering window they're using.

**There's a second way to reach the Gathering Areas: Reiko Kurogane's wagon service.** It skips the RAP cost and the Road Encounter roll entirely — for a price that looks like 20% of that trip's haul and is very often more, once the fine print is read. See `13`, "Reiko's Wagon Concern," for the full contract and how it actually plays out.

**Road Encounters** (`10`) are rolled once per one-way trip — a round trip to a Gathering Area rolls it once heading out and once heading back (twice total).

## Free Spaces — d10

Scattered through Residential & Market, the Slums, and the Old Path — 8–10 of these across the village, DM's placement. Roll once per Free Space to seed it, or leave blank and use as-needed.

| d10 | Seed |
|---|---|
| 1 | Abandoned market stall — could be claimed/repaired for a Build/Expand-style bonus |
| 2 | Overgrown garden patch — minor one-time forage flavor pickup, not a full Node |
| 3 | Village noticeboard — where flyers and rumors are posted |
| 4 | Old well — mostly flavor, a good quiet meeting spot |
| 5 | Boarded-up house — rumor says a family left after "trouble" with the Syndicate |
| 6 | Kids playing — a Young Boy/Young Girl encounter (`06`) |
| 7 | A busker's favorite corner — Performance-friendly (`04`, `12`) |
| 8 | Guard's rest post — a friendly guard NPC willing to chat |
| 9 | Stray cat colony — a soft quest lead (`09`) |
| 10 | Empty and forgettable — reserve for a future Daily Event |

**DM Note:** Free Spaces are also the easiest place to slot in an NPC interaction roll (`06`) when a player wanders somewhere with no fixed content.
