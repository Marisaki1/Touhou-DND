# FLOOR 8: THE MERCHANT'S CROSSING
### Part 6 — NPC Interaction Tables

Every rollable NPC in town — in a Common House, at a Free Space, in the Slums, at the Church, wandering per the Whereabouts table (`05`) — belongs to one of eight archetypes. When a player interacts with an NPC and you don't already have something specific planned, pick (or roll d8) an archetype and roll on its table below.

**d8 Archetype Die** (if you want randomness instead of picking): 1 Adult Man · 2 Adult Female · 3 Teenage Boy · 4 Teenage Girl · 5 Young Boy · 6 Young Girl · 7 Old Man · 8 Old Woman

**How to roll:** most tables are d10. Use the Round Action **Talk to NPC** (1 RAP, no check) for a guaranteed flavor-tier result, or spend the appropriate check (Persuasion, Insight, Investigation, etc., DM's call based on the approach) to roll properly and access the higher-value Trade/Clue/Favor results.

**GP values** referenced below (e.g., "Trade — Small tier GP") are defined precisely in `07`. As a quick reminder of the shape: Young NPCs never hand over gold — they trade in items, secrets, and quest leads. Teenagers deal in pocket change. Adults are the main trading partners. Elders occasionally offer the single richest payout in the whole table, but rarely, and it's gated behind a proper Favor.

**Do a Favor** (Major Action, 6 rounds, Persuasion or Insight DC 12, once per NPC) is the only way to access a table's "Favor" row. Everything else here is a Round Action.

## ADULT MAN — d10

| d10 | Result | Type | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hauling firewood or crates, gives a tired nod | Flavor | Color only |
| 2 | Offers to swap a Small-tier raw material for one you're carrying | Trade | Item-for-item, no gold |
| 3 | Mentions a supply wagon arrived late this week | Clue | 50% genuine Market Trend hint, 50% gossip about a rival team's stall |
| 4 | Needs help patching a fence or roof, Athletics DC 11 | Favor | Success: Small item + Reputation +1 |
| 5 | Buys a Small or Medium item off you at listed price, no haggling | Trade | Pays the archetype's Trade GP (`07`) |
| 6 | Grumbles about "some ledger fellow" sniffing around his neighbor's shop | Clue | Counts as a Syndicate Tip lead — feeds Investigate Syndicate or the hidden storyline (`14`) |
| 7 | Curt and untrusting — got fined recently by someone "official-looking" | Flavor/Gate | Refuses further interaction unless your Reputation is 0 or higher |
| 8 | Friendly regular, if you've helped him before | Trade/Clue | Standing 10% discount on anything he sells you, plus one free Clue result (roll again, reroll an 8) |
| 9 | Head of a Large household, keeps a watchdog nearby | Flavor/Deterrent | Only appears at Large houses. A quiet warning shot: this house is guarded. Perception DC 11 notices the dog's alertness. |
| 10 | Special — DM discretion, unique to this NPC | Special | Build something specific to this house/story beat |

## Quick DM Reference — What Each Archetype Is *For*

| Archetype | Best For |
|---|---|
| Adult Man / Adult Female | Bread-and-butter trades, moderate GP, Syndicate Tip clues |
| Teenage Boy / Teenage Girl | Light gossip, small favors, scouting-adjacent clues, Cat Herder side-hooks |
| Young Boy / Young Girl | Zero-GP color, guaranteed Cat Herder leads, Free Space reveals, softened Syndicate hints |
| Old Man / Old Woman | The richest single payouts (via Favor only), the *best* hidden-storyline clues, and Large-house gravitas |
