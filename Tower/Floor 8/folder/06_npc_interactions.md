# FLOOR 8: THE HUMAN VILLAGE
### Part 6 — NPC Interaction Tables

Every rollable NPC in the village — in a house, at a Free Space, in the Slums, wandering per the Whereabouts table (`05`) — belongs to one of eight archetypes. When a player interacts with an NPC and you don't already have something specific planned, pick (or roll d8) an archetype and use its menu below.

**d8 Archetype Die:** 1 Adult Man · 2 Adult Woman · 3 Teenage Boy · 4 Teenage Girl · 5 Young Boy · 6 Young Girl · 7 Old Man · 8 Old Woman

## The Three Things You Can Do With an NPC

- **Give Info** (Round Action, 1 RAP, no check for a flavor-tier answer; a proper skill check per the DM's call on approach unlocks the table's better rows) — roll on that archetype's Info Table below.
- **Trade Item** (Round Action, 1 RAP) — the NPC buys one item off you at its assessed value, no haggling needed. Persuasion DC 12 pushes it 10% higher. Only **Adult Man, Adult Woman, Old Man, and Old Woman** can do this — each has a hard **lifetime gold cap** (below). Once an NPC has paid out their cap, they're tapped out for the rest of the floor — still good for Info, just no more gold.
- **Do a Favor** (Major Action, 1 MAP, Persuasion or Insight DC 12, once per NPC) — completes a task for the NPC. Pays out in items and Reputation, never gold, **except** for Teenagers, whose Favor is the one place their pocket money changes hands.

### Gold Caps — Quick Reference

| Archetype | Gold Cap | Can Trade Items? | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adult Man | **40gp** | Yes | The village's bread-and-butter buyer |
| Adult Woman | **60gp** | Yes | The best buyer in the village — and where Kazuo's jewelry does the most damage (`14`) |
| Old Man / Old Woman | **30gp** | Yes | Lower cap than the Adults, but the richest Info |
| Teenage Boy / Teenage Girl | 20gp (on hand) | **No** — will never buy your goods | Their 20gp only ever changes hands through a Favor, or if they're robbed |
| Young Boy / Young Girl | 0gp, always | Barter only — item-for-item, no gold either way | Never carry tradeable gold, full stop |

## ADULT MAN — 40gp cap, can Trade

Menu: Give Info · Trade Item · **Ask About Crafting** (guaranteed answer — see below) · Do a Favor

**Ask About Crafting:** any player who directly asks an Adult Man whether crafting is possible gets a straight answer: *"Guild Hall'll do it, if you've got the pieces for it. Ask for the workshop."* No roll needed — this is the only way the Craft mechanic (`09`) is ever revealed to players.

### Info Table — d6

| d6 | Result |
|---|---|
| 1 | Mentions a supply wagon arrived late this week — a genuine Market Trend hint (`12`) |
| 2 | Gossip about a rival team's stall |
| 3 | Grumbles about "the collector" doing his rounds again — a Syndicate Tip (`13`) |
| 4 | Points out a house that's currently empty, and why (see `05` Whereabouts) |
| 5 | Curt and untrusting — got fined recently by "someone official-looking." Refuses further Info unless Reputation is 0 or higher |
| 6 | *(Day 4+ only)* Lowers his voice — "there's a fella been seen near the edge of town, quiet, always got crows on him somewhere." A Kazuo Clue (`14`) |

**At a Large house:** an Adult Man here is the household head, and keeps a watchdog nearby — Perception DC 11 notices the dog's alertness before attempting a robbery.

## TEENAGE BOY — 20gp on hand, cannot Trade

Menu: Give Info · Do a Favor · Hire for an Errand

**Do a Favor** works exactly like the Adult/Old version (Major Action, 1 MAP, Persuasion or Insight DC 12, once per NPC) — except a Teenager's Favor is the *only* place their 20gp changes hands at all, since they never Trade. This is deliberate: a Teenager can still pay you for real help, they just won't buy your goods.

**Hire for an Errand** (Round Action, 2 RAP, no check) runs the other direction — *you* pay *him* a small tip (player's choice, typically 1–3gp) to save yourself a Travel action. This costs the player gold; it doesn't touch his 20gp cap or the Town Gold Pool either way.

### Info Table — d6

| d6 | Result |
|---|---|
| 1 | Brags about sneaking somewhere he shouldn't have — 50% genuine tip about an unguarded spot, 50% exaggeration |
| 2 | Saw someone "shady" near the Guild or the Slums — a Syndicate Tip (`13`) |
| 3 | Has helped you before, now tags along and points out things — free Scout Rival Stall-equivalent result (`04`), no action cost |
| 4 | Dares another kid to sneak near a Large house at night — a soft lead on that house's deterrent |
| 5 | Mouthy, tries to get a rise out of you — Intimidation used here costs Reputation −1 if witnessed |
| 6 | *(Day 4+ only)* "My aunt got weird after she bought some necklace off a stall. Nobody believes me." A Kazuo Clue (`14`) |

## OLD MAN — 30gp cap, can Trade

Menu: Give Info (the best in the village) · Trade Item · Do a Favor (once per NPC — physically demanding, but pays generously in items)

**Favor rewards here aren't limited to generic items** — an Old Man who's asked for real help can just as easily hand over a cart-upgrade component (`01`), a letter of introduction (a soft price break with a specific rival stall), or a piece of practical know-how (advantage on a specific future check), DM's discretion based on what the Favor actually was. The point is players shouldn't need gold to get useful things done — Elders are where that shows up most.

### Info Table — d6

| d6 | Result |
|---|---|
| 1 | Remembers what the market looked like before the Syndicate took its cut — flavor, but a good rapport-builder |
| 2 | Recalls a name tied to the Syndicate's local rise — a strong Syndicate Tip (`13`) |
| 3 | Offers a genuine blessing of experience — Advantage on your next Persuasion or Insight check today with any Old-archetype NPC |
| 4 | Set in his ways, doesn't trust "new faces with new prices" — refuses further Info unless your Reputation is +1 or higher |
| 5 | Points out a house's schedule cold (`05`) |
| 6 | *(Day 4+ only)* Goes very quiet, then: "there's an old story about servants who trade in years instead of coin. I didn't think it was real." The single best Kazuo Clue on this table (`14`) |

**At a Large house:** an Old Man here was once someone important in the village. A respectful approach (Persuasion or Insight DC 13) rather than a robbery goes a long way — see `14` for what that can unlock once the Kazuo storyline is live.

## Quick DM Reference — What Each Archetype Is *For*

| Archetype | Best For |
|---|---|
| Adult Man / Adult Woman | Bread-and-butter trades, the only source of the Crafting reveal, Syndicate Tips |
| Teenage Boy / Teenage Girl | Light gossip, small paid errands, scouting-adjacent clues |
| Young Boy / Young Girl | Zero-gold color, quest leads, Free Space reveals, softened Kazuo hints |
| Old Man / Old Woman | The richest Info, the *best* Kazuo Clues, and Large-house gravitas |
