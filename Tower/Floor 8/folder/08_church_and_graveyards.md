# FLOOR 8: THE MERCHANT'S CROSSING
### Part 8 — The Church & The Graveyards

## THE CHURCH

A quiet stone building north of the Market Square. Home to a small clergy, a collection plate, and the locked door to the Pristine Graveyard behind it.

**Church Almsgiving** (Round Action, 2 RAP, Religion DC 12) — assisting with a service, reading a sermon, or simply being seen doing good work.

| Result | Reward |
|---|---|
| Fail | Nothing, no cost |
| Success | 1d4gp |
| Success by 5+ | 2d4gp + Reputation +1 |
| Nat 20 | 3d4gp + Reputation +1 + a minor blessing (DM discretion — e.g., advantage on your next Persuasion check today) |

### Church NPC Table — d6

Clergy and visitors here skew Old Man/Old Woman (senior clergy) or Adult (acolytes, mourners) — feel free to reroll a d6 result here into the matching archetype table in `06` if you want a fuller interaction instead of the summary below.

| d6 | Result |
|---|---|
| 1 | The priest (Old Man/Old Woman) offers a blessing — flavor, or +1 to your next Persuasion check with an elder-type NPC today |
| 2 | A grieving family member (Adult) — a Do a Favor hook |
| 3 | An acolyte (Teenage Boy/Girl) gossips about who's been visiting the graveyard oddly often — a soft warning that rivals may be eyeing the Pristine Graveyard |
| 4 | A wary clergy member is watching the party closely — Insight DC 12 to notice you're being watched, relevant if you're about to attempt Sneak the Church |
| 5 | A traveling pilgrim (Adult) shares a rumor |
| 6 | Quiet; nothing of note today |

## THE PRISTINE GRAVEYARD

A walled plot behind the Church, reserved for the town's oldest and wealthiest families. **This is a one-time, floor-wide prize** — once looted, by any team, it's gone for the rest of the floor. All four teams are effectively racing for it.

**Stage 1 — Sneak the Church:** Major Action, 3 rounds, Stealth DC 14. Failure: caught mid-church — Reputation −3 immediately, and Alertness (shared) +3, even before reaching the graveyard.

**Stage 2 — The Heist:** Major Action, 8 rounds, Stealth or Sleight of Hand DC 15.

| Result | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Success | 35gp in grave goods and valuables (draws from the Town Economy Pool). The Pristine Graveyard is now looted — remove it from play. |
| Nat 20 | 60gp — a hidden reliquary. Still closes the location out for the floor. |
| Failure | Nothing gained. **Sacrilege consequence** (below) triggers instead of the standard Getting Caught table. |
| Failure by 5+ | Same as above, but Alertness gain is doubled. |

**Sacrilege Consequence (replaces Getting Caught for this location):** Alertness (shared) +8, Reputation −4, and the clergy formally reports the team to the Guild — a Guard NPC visits their stall during the next Selling Phase specifically to "ask about their whereabouts," costing 1 customer slot.

## THE COMMON GRAVEYARD

Public, unwalled, no sneaking required. Mourners leave offerings; headstones crumble; there's always something worth picking through.

**Salvage the Common Graveyard** — Major Action, 5 rounds, Investigation or Religion DC 12, repeatable daily.

Roll d6 on success:

| d6 | Result |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | 1 Small or Medium-tier raw material, player's choice of table |
| 5–6 | 1d4gp found offering (draws from the Town Economy Pool) |

Small risk: on a natural 1 on the check itself, a mourner (roll on `06` for who — usually an Old Man/Old Woman tending a grave, or an Adult mourner) witnesses the disturbance — Reputation −1.
