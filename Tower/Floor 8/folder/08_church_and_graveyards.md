# FLOOR 8: THE HUMAN VILLAGE
### Part 8 — The Graveyards

Two graveyards sit on the outskirts of Residential & Market (`02`). Grave robbing is a legitimate, if morally gray, way to make money on this floor — it doesn't need to be dressed up as anything more than that.

## THE PRISTINE GRAVEYARD

A walled plot reserved for the village's oldest and wealthiest families, watched over loosely by a couple of caretakers. **This is a one-time, floor-wide prize** — once looted, by any team, it's gone for the rest of the floor. All four teams are effectively racing for it.

**Stage 1 — Sneak Past the Watchers:** Major Action, 1 MAP, Stealth DC 14. Failure: caught mid-approach — Reputation −3 immediately, and Alertness (shared) +3, even before reaching the graves.

**Stage 2 — The Heist:** Major Action, 2 MAP, Stealth or Sleight of Hand DC 15.

| Result | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Success | 40gp in grave goods and valuables (draws from the Town Gold Pool, `01`). The Pristine Graveyard is now looted — remove it from play. |
| Nat 20 | 70gp — a hidden reliquary. Still closes the location out for the floor. |
| Failure | Nothing gained. **Caretaker's Alarm** (below) triggers instead of the standard Getting Caught table (`05`). |
| Failure by 5+ | Same as above, but Alertness gain is doubled. |

**Caretaker's Alarm (replaces Getting Caught for this location):** Alertness (shared) +8, Reputation −4, and a Guard NPC visits the offending team's stall during the next Selling Phase specifically to "ask about their whereabouts," costing 1 customer slot.

## THE PUBLIC GRAVEYARD

Unwalled, no sneaking required. Mourners leave offerings; headstones crumble; there's always something worth picking through.

**Salvage the Public Graveyard** — Major Action, 1 MAP, Investigation or Religion DC 12, repeatable daily.

Roll d6 on success:

| d6 | Result |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | 1 Small or Medium-tier raw material, player's choice of table |
| 5–6 | 1d4gp found offering (draws from the Town Gold Pool) |

Small risk: on a natural 1 on the check itself, a mourner (roll on `06` for who — usually an Old Man/Old Woman or an Adult) witnesses the disturbance — Reputation −1.
