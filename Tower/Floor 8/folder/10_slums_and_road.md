# FLOOR 8: THE HUMAN VILLAGE
### Part 10 — The Slums & The Roads

## THE SLUMS

The connecting district between the wild (Wildlife Forest, Mining Cave) and Residential & Market (`02`). Rows of houses here — none enterable. A handful of fixed, recurring spots carry all the mechanical weight; the rest is atmosphere.

| Spot | Action | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Soup Kitchen** | Charity — donate Small/Medium goods or 10gp+ | Major Action, 1 MAP | Reputation +2 |
| **The Fence** | Sell robbed/stolen goods off the books | Round Action, 1 RAP | 60–70% of base value, paid instantly, no Reputation risk, bypasses the Masked White Girl entirely — but +1 Syndicate Pressure per use (the Fence answers to the Syndicate, `13`) |
| **The Fight Ring** | Underground brawling for quick coin | Major Action, 1 MAP, contested Athletics or combat vs. a Syndicate Enforcer statline opponent | Win: 2d6gp. Lose: no gold, minor HP loss, no lasting penalty |
| **Rumor Alley** | Cheap gossip network | Round Action, 1 RAP, Investigation DC 10 | 1 clue, cheaper and faster than town-side Investigate actions but lower quality |

### Slums NPC Table — d8

The Slums have fewer named NPCs than the village proper, and they don't carry gold to trade — this is where **Performance, Charity, and morally gray opportunities** live instead.

| d8 | Result | Type | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A tired Adult watching the road, willing to talk if approached kindly | Flavor/Clue | 50% genuine rumor about Syndicate activity (`13`), 50% just weariness |
| 2 | A group of Young kids daring each other near the Fight Ring | Flavor | Color only, or a Cat Herder-style lead if you need one |
| 3 | An Old Man or Old Woman who's lived here a long time | Clue | A strong lead — the Slums are a good place to find witnesses too poor for the Syndicate to bother with |
| 4 | A Teenage runner who knows the Fence's schedule cold | Flavor/Trade | Can get you into the Fence one time without the usual wait, no cost |
| 5 | A performer busking for scraps | Flavor | A Performance check here (DC 10) earns 1d4gp directly — one of the only ways to earn coin in the Slums outside the fixed spots (still counts toward the once-per-day Busk cap, `12`) |
| 6 | Someone offering "work" that's clearly not legal | Flavor/Hook | DM discretion — a seed for a gray-morality side path; never required |
| 7 | A desperate Adult asking for real help, not charity | Favor | Major Action, 1 MAP, Insight or Persuasion DC 13 — success: Reputation +2 and a Slum-specific ally who can pass future rumors reliably |
| 8 | Quiet; the road takes people through without incident today | Flavor | Nothing of note |

## THE ROADS

Wildlife Forest and Mining Cave share a western road through the Slums. Forest Forage has its own southern path, past the Syndicate house. Any Travel action targeting a Gathering Area risks an encounter.

**Road Encounter Check:** roll 1d20 each time a party travels. On a 1–3, roll on the table below.

| d6 | Encounter |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Bandit Ambush** — a small group tries to rob the party's carried items (max 5 per Rule 8, `01`). Combat, or Intimidation/Persuasion to avoid. Completes the Bandit Patrol Guild quest if active. |
| 2 | **Fellow Traveler** — a friendly NPC merchant or pilgrim (roll `06` for a full interaction); chance for a quick trade or gossip |
| 3 | **Broken Cart** (an NPC's, not yours) — helping (Athletics or Persuasion) grants minor Reputation or a small item |
| 4 | **Rival Team Sighting** — another team's party is also on the road; pure tension, DM discretion |
| 5 | **Toll/Shakedown** — low-level Syndicate-adjacent thugs demand 2–5gp; pay, or contest with Intimidation/Combat |
| 6 | **Nothing** — the road is quiet |
