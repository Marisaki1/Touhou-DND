# FLOOR 8: THE MERCHANT'S CROSSING
### Part 10 — The Slums & The Road

## THE SLUMS

Beyond the town's edge, along the Road. Over a hundred houses — none of them enterable. A handful of fixed, recurring spots carry all the mechanical weight here; the rest is atmosphere.

| Spot | Action | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Soup Kitchen** | Charity — donate Small/Medium goods or 10gp+ | Major Action, 3 rounds | Reputation +2 |
| **The Fence** | Sell robbed/stolen goods off the books | Round Action, 1 RAP | 60–70% of base value, paid instantly, no Reputation risk, bypasses Soren entirely — but +1 Syndicate Pressure per use (the Fence is Iron Ledger-adjacent) |
| **The Fight Ring** | Underground brawling for quick coin | Major Action, 4 rounds, contested Athletics or combat vs. a Syndicate Enforcer statline opponent | Win: 2d6gp. Lose: no gold, minor HP loss, no lasting penalty |
| **Rumor Alley** | Cheap gossip network | Round Action, 1 RAP, Investigation DC 10 | 1 clue, cheaper and faster than town-side Investigate actions but lower quality |

### Slums NPC Table — d8

The Slums have far fewer named NPCs than town proper, and they don't carry gold to trade — this is where **Performance, Charity, and morally gray (or outright unsavory) opportunities** live instead. When an NPC does appear here, treat their archetype loosely — the table below already builds the flavor in, so you don't need to cross-reference `06` unless you want to.

| d8 | Result | Type | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A tired Adult watching the road, willing to talk if approached kindly | Flavor/Clue | 50% genuine rumor about Syndicate activity (feeds `13`/`14`), 50% just weariness |
| 2 | A group of Young kids daring each other near the Fight Ring | Flavor | Color only, or a Cat Herder-style lead if you need one |
| 3 | An Old Man or Old Woman who remembers the town before the Ledger | Clue | A strong, specific hidden-storyline lead (`14`) — the Slums are a good place to find witnesses too poor or too forgotten for the Iron Ledger to bother silencing |
| 4 | A Teenage runner who knows the Fence's schedule cold | Flavor/Trade | Can get you into the Fence one time without the usual queue/wait, no cost |
| 5 | A performer busking for scraps | Flavor | A Performance check here (DC 10) earns 1d4gp directly from a small crowd — one of the only ways to earn coin in the Slums outside the fixed spots |
| 6 | Someone offering "work" that's clearly not legal | Flavor/Hook | DM discretion — a seed for an evil/gray-morality side path (fencing extra goods, intimidation-for-hire, etc.); never required |
| 7 | A desperate Adult asking for real help, not charity | Favor | Major Action, 4 rounds, Insight or Persuasion DC 13 — success: Reputation +2 and a Slum-specific ally who can pass future rumors reliably |
| 8 | Quiet; the Road takes people through without incident today | Flavor | Nothing of note |

## THE ROAD

A single route running from the Gathering Areas (west), through town, out to the Slums (east). Any Travel action targeting a Gathering Area or the Slums counts as travel "on the Road" and risks an encounter.

**Road Encounter Check:** roll 1d20 each time a party travels the Road. On a 1–3, roll on the table below.

| d6 | Encounter |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Bandit Ambush** — a small group tries to rob the party's carried items (max 5 per Rule 8). Combat, or Intimidation/Persuasion to avoid. Completes the Bandit Patrol Guild quest if active. |
| 2 | **Fellow Traveler** — a friendly NPC merchant or pilgrim (roll `06` if you want a full interaction); chance for a quick trade or gossip |
| 3 | **Broken Cart** (an NPC's, not yours) — helping (Athletics or Persuasion) grants minor Reputation or a small item |
| 4 | **Rival Team Sighting** — another team's party is also on the Road; pure tension, DM discretion |
| 5 | **Toll/Shakedown** — low-level Syndicate-adjacent thugs demand 2–5gp; pay, or contest with Intimidation/Combat |
| 6 | **Nothing** — the Road is quiet |
