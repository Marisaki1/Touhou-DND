# FLOOR 8: THE MERCHANT'S CROSSING
### Part 12 — Reputation & The Selling Phase

## REPUTATION TRACKER

Starts at **0**, range **−10 to +10**. Governs customer count, base sale price, and which negotiation tools are available.

| Score | Customer Modifier | Base Price Modifier | Tool Access | Deception Discovery Modifier |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **+6 to +10** | +1d6 customers; vendors 15% better; 1 guaranteed Special Customer | +15% | All tools | −15% discovery |
| **+3 to +5** | +1d4 customers; vendors 5% better | +8% | All tools | −8% |
| **+1 to +2** | minor positive recognition | +3% | All tools | −3% |
| **0** | neutral | — | All tools | — |
| **−1 to −2** | slight wariness | −5% | Luxury Upsell DC +2 | +5% |
| **−3 to −5** | −1d4 customers; 2 vendors refuse to sell to you | −12% | Luxury Upsell unavailable; Upsell DC +2 | +12% |
| **−6 to −9** | −1d6 customers; authority watching | −25% | Negotiation locked — Honest Sale only | +20% |
| **−10** | stall shut down 1 full day | — | Cannot sell | — |

Stacks with Market Trend (e.g., Liked category at +6 Reputation ≈ +45%; Disliked category at −6 Reputation ≈ half price).

### Reputation Gains
Honest sale to elder/authority +1 · Public performance DC15+ +1 · Resolving a dispute fairly +1 · Donating goods (10gp+) +2 · Publicly exposing a Syndicate agent via Soren +2 · Praised by The Critic +2 · Publicly exposing a Mark-Hunter +1 · Completing a Slum Outreach or Soup Kitchen charity action +2–3 (see `09`/`10`) · Helping an NPC via a Favor result on `06` +1 (most rows)

### Reputation Losses
Failed deception discovered −1 · Intimidating a witnessed customer −1 (cumulative/day) · Rumor traced to you −2 · Theft discovered −3 · Witnessed bribe −1 · Violence inside the bazaar −2/incident · Losing patience with a Mark-Hunter (witnessed) −1

## PRICE MANIPULATION — Persuasion, Deception, Intimidation

Once per customer during the Selling Phase. Apply Tool Access restrictions and Deception Discovery Modifier from the Reputation Tracker on top of these base DCs.

| Method | Skill | DC | On Success | On Failure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Honest Sale | — | — | Listed price guaranteed | — |
| Upsell (≤30%) | Persuasion | 10 + markup% | Full marked-up price | Base price only |
| Luxury Upsell (31–60%) | Persuasion | 14 + markup% | Full marked-up price | Customer walks (d6: 5–6, buys from a rival) |
| Storytelling | Performance | 12 | +15% flat, stacks with Upsell | No bonus |
| Misrepresent Quality | Deception | 14 | Full inflated price now | Caught on the spot — sale fails, Rep −1, next 1d4 customers wary |
| Misrepresent Rarity | Deception | 15 | Full inflated price now | Caught on the spot — sale fails, Rep −2, same wariness |
| Pressure Sell | Intimidation | 15 | Full price guaranteed | −1d4 customers rest of phase |

**Pressure Sell:** Reputation −1 per use, cumulative same day, regardless of outcome.

**Deception discovery** (checked once at end of day, per successful lie): Misrepresent Quality 25% base / Misrepresent Rarity 35% base — apply the Reputation Tracker's Discovery Modifier. If discovered: refund the sale's gold, and Reputation −1 (Quality) or −2 (Rarity).

## MARKET TRENDS → PRICE

Each morning: **1 Liked (+30%), 2 Neutral, 1 Disliked (−25%)** across Hunt/Fish/Forage/Mine. Affects sale price only, never customer count or gathering. Hidden — read via Observe/Scout Rival Stall, Investigate Market Trend, or Bram/Celeste/Garrath (`16`).

## THE SELLING PHASE

6:00–10:00. Not round-counted — runs until that day's customers are processed.

### How Many Customers Per Day

| Day | Suggested Base |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | 1d4+3 (4–7) |
| Day 2 | 1d6+3 (4–9) |
| Day 3 | 1d6+3, −1d4 all stalls (Grey Ledger opens) |
| Day 4 | 1d6+4 (5–10) |
| Day 5 | 1d8+4 (5–12); +2d8 if Vael is defeated |

Modifiers stack on top: signs +1d4, demo +2d4, shout +1d4, street crier +1d6, masterwork display +1d6, Reputation per tracker, Slanderer −1d6, rival sabotaged +1d4, Grey Ledger −1d4.

Resolve routine customers (village child, farm laborer, etc.) with a quick price-and-roll; fully roleplay Special Customers and Syndicate interruptions.
