# FLOOR 8: THE HUMAN VILLAGE
### Part 13 — The Syndicate

## What The Syndicate Actually Is

No hidden entity, no ancient pact, no ledger with a mind of its own. **The Syndicate controls the Human Village's trade** — full stop. They started as toll collectors on the roads generations back and never stopped. Every stall that's operated here long-term pays them something, one way or another: a cut of profits, a "protection" fee, a favor owed. They don't own the market. They just make sure nobody forgets they could make things difficult if they wanted to.

They are not subtle about existing, but they are careful about being provoked directly — most villagers have simply built the Syndicate's cut into how they think about doing business here, the way anyone gets used to a bad toll road.

**For the players, this means:** the Syndicate is a recurring nuisance and a recurring opportunity. They'll lean on whichever team is doing best. They'll also deal, if a team is willing to play along — see the Syndicate as a possible route to tracking Kazuo (`14`), if a team's stayed on their good side.

## Guaranteed Daily Pressure

From **Pressure 4 (Active) and above**, the Syndicate does *something* against the players every single day, whether or not the normal Selling Phase Interruption roll (below) ever triggers.

**At the end of any day where Pressure is 4+ and no Interruption fired that day**, roll once:

### Background Malice — d6

| d6 | Result |
|---|---|
| 1 | A rumor about "trouble at the market" makes the rounds — flavor only |
| 2 | A supplier quietly declines to sell to the leading stall tomorrow morning until a Persuasion DC 12 smooths it over |
| 3 | One house NPC who was Friendly becomes wary instead — someone had a word with them |
| 4 | The Syndicate Stall (Day 3+, if open, below) restocks early and undercuts one category by an extra 10% |
| 5 | A Guard patrol is quietly redirected — Alertness gain from robbery is reduced by 1 for the next day only, because the Syndicate wants houses more exposed to something else entirely (DM discretion) |
| 6 | Nothing overt — narrate a small unsettling detail (a coin left tarnished black on a stall counter, a name scratched into a post) as pure atmosphere |

### Grudge Track — per team, 0–10

How much heat a *specific* team has drawn.

**Increases:** publicly exposing an agent at your stall +2 · leading all stalls by 20gp+ +1 · sabotaging the Syndicate Stall +2
**Decreases:** a full day with no antagonism −1 · paying a Tax Collector's fee without resistance −1

| Grudge | Behavior Toward This Team |
|---|---|
| 0–2 | Baseline — treated like anyone else |
| 3–5 | Targeted — 50% chance any triggered interruption redirects to this team specifically |
| 6–8 | Marked — a continuous Slanderer campaign runs: −1d4 customers every day until countered |
| 9–10 | Open Hostility — a Tax Collector visits daily without exception; Reputation cannot rise above +3 while Grudge stays this high |

## Syndicate Harassment Isn't Only At Night

The Syndicate doesn't wait for the Selling Phase. During the Production Phase:

- **The Mining Cave** carries a 1-in-6 chance per Explore trip of a Syndicate patrol, unless Stealth DC 13 is passed before descending (`03`).
- **The roads** roll Syndicate-adjacent tolls and shakedowns on the Road Encounter table (`10`).
- **Robbery** that gets caught can escalate straight to Syndicate attention (`05`'s Getting Caught table, result 4).
- **Any daytime activity the DM wants to complicate** can have a Syndicate collector simply show up — demanding a cut of whatever was just gathered, crafted, or earned, especially against a team with Grudge 3+.

## Reiko's Wagon Concern

Reiko Kurogane (`17`) owns more than the back room she negotiates from. **The Kurogane Carriage Concern** runs the wagons that ferry teams out to the Gathering Areas and back — a real, legitimate-looking service, a booth and a driver and everything, parked at the edge of Residential & Market every morning.

**What it actually does, mechanically:** a team that rides the Concern's wagon instead of walking skips the standard travel cost for that trip entirely — **no RAP spent on the hop out, no Road Encounter roll** (`02`, `10`). It's a genuine convenience, and it's meant to feel like one. The Gather action's own 3 MAP is unaffected either way; the wagon only replaces the walk.

**The price is a contract**, and the contract is where the trick lives.

### The Posted Terms (what players see first)

A hand-lettered board at the booth, friendly and simple:

> *"Ride safe. Ride fast. Twenty percent of what you bring back — that's all. Sign below, hop on."*

Most parties sign off this alone. The full written contract only comes out if someone specifically asks to see it before signing (Investigation or Insight DC 15 to think to ask, or simply player-initiated caution) — the driver hands it over without protest if asked, because handing it over isn't the trick; reading past the first page is.

### THE FULL CONTRACT (hand this to players who ask to read it, or read it aloud)

> **TERMS OF CARRIAGE — KUROGANE CARRIAGE CONCERN**
>
> **Gathering Route Service, entered into between the Concern and the undersigned Bearer(s)**
>
> &nbsp;
>
> **1. DEFINITIONS**
>
> 1.1 *"Gathered Goods"* — anything harvested, extracted, caught, or foraged by the Bearer(s) during the covered excursion, without regard to quality, condition, or the Bearer's intent to sell, keep, or otherwise dispose of the same.
>
> 1.2 *"Assessed Value"* — the value of Gathered Goods as determined by the Concern's collector at the point of Settlement, consistent with prevailing market rates as the Concern understands them.
>
> &nbsp;
>
> **2. CARRIAGE FEE**
>
> 2.1 The Bearer shall render unto the Concern, at the conclusion of the covered excursion, a sum equal to twenty percent (20%) of the Assessed Value of all Gathered Goods transported that excursion.
>
> &nbsp;
>
> **3. FORM OF SETTLEMENT**
>
> 3.1 Settlement may be rendered in coin, in full, at the time of Assessment, provided such coin is tendered by the Bearer without prompting from the collector.
>
> 3.2 Where coin sufficient to satisfy Clause 2.1 is not tendered under 3.1, Settlement shall instead be rendered in Gathered Goods, the specific item or items to be selected **at the sole discretion of the Concern's collector**, without regard to the Bearer's preference as to which goods are surrendered.
>
> 3.3 Where the Bearer's Gathered Goods cannot be apportioned such that any combination thereof precisely equals the sum owed under Clause 2.1, the collector shall select whichever whole, undivided item or items **most nearly satisfy the debt without falling short of it.**
>
> 3.4 No change, credit, refund, or partial return shall be rendered where the value of goods surrendered under 3.2–3.3 exceeds the sum owed. Any such excess is considered a gratuity, freely and knowingly given, for which the Concern extends its thanks.
>
> &nbsp;
>
> **4. SUPPLEMENTARY CHARGES**
>
> 4.1 A wagon maintenance surcharge of 1gp per excursion applies, assessed prior to the calculation under Clause 2.1.
>
> 4.2 Excursions boarding more than five minutes past the scheduled hour incur a delay accommodation charge, at the collector's discretion.
>
> &nbsp;
>
> **5. VALUATION AND DISPUTE**
>
> 5.1 The Concern's Assessed Value, once rendered, is final.
>
> 5.2 A Bearer wishing to dispute an Assessed Value may pursue the Concern's arbitration process, the cost of which is borne by the disputing party regardless of outcome.
>
> &nbsp;
>
> **6. SIGNATURE**
>
> *By boarding the wagon, the Bearer(s) affirm they have read and accept these Terms in full.*

### How This Actually Plays Out (DM-only)

The trap is entirely in **Clause 3** — payment must come out of whatever the team physically has on hand, and gathered items aren't divisible. A team that gathered, say, a Medium item (6gp) and a Large item (12gp) — 18gp total, owing 20% = 3.6gp — almost never has exact coin on them after a day of harvesting. Per 3.2–3.4, the collector takes the *smallest whole item that covers the debt without falling short*: the 6gp Medium item, in full, no change. **Effective rate paid: 6 ÷ 18 = 33%**, not 20% — and that's a mild example. A team with only one item to their name that trip hands over the whole thing, whatever its value.

**The honest way out:** a team that keeps a little spare coin specifically for this — even just enough to cover 20% of a typical haul — can pay per Clause 3.1 and get the actual advertised rate. The contract isn't unbeatable; it's exploiting the fact that a team returning from a Gather action is carrying goods, not coin, and the friction of that is where the real money comes from.

**Once a team gets fleeced once, they know.** From then on, riding the wagon is an informed choice, not a trap — some teams will keep using it anyway for the convenience and just budget for the real rate; others will start walking, eat the RAP cost and the Road Encounter risk, and resent Reiko a little more for it.

**If a team calls it out:**
- **Confronting the driver directly** gets nowhere — they're not authorized to renegotiate anything, and genuinely don't think they've done anything wrong ("it's all in the contract, friend").
- **Persuasion DC 16 with Reiko herself** (`17`), in person, gets the team the revised contract below — see it as her conceding the point, not admitting fault: *"Most people just stop riding. You're the first to ask for better terms instead."*
- **Using this as Evidence toward exposing Reiko's business practices** doesn't function like exposing a Selling Phase enemy (`13`'s Expose options) — the contract is, technically, legal, and Reiko never claimed otherwise. It's a good Investigation angle for flavor and Reputation with *other villagers* who've been burned by it too (DM discretion, small Reputation gain for publicizing it at, say, Rumor Alley, `10`), but it doesn't reduce Pressure or Grudge on its own.

### THE REVISED CONTRACT (if a team earns it)

A team that beats Reiko at Persuasion DC 16 doesn't just get a verbal promise — she has a second contract already drawn up, because this isn't the first time it's happened and it won't be the last. She produces it without comment, has it signed, and the matter is closed.

> **TERMS OF CARRIAGE — KUROGANE CARRIAGE CONCERN**
>
> **Gathering Route Service, Revised Terms, entered into between the Concern and the undersigned Bearer(s)**
>
> &nbsp;
>
> **1. DEFINITIONS**
>
> 1.1 *"Gathered Goods"* — anything harvested, extracted, caught, or foraged by the Bearer(s) during the covered excursion.
>
> 1.2 *"Assessed Value"* — the base value of Gathered Goods as independently identified by the Tower's appraiser, consulted by either party if a figure is in question. Neither party's own estimate is binding over the other's.
>
> &nbsp;
>
> **2. CARRIAGE FEE**
>
> 2.1 The Bearer shall render unto the Concern, at the conclusion of the covered excursion, a sum equal to twenty percent (20%) of the Assessed Value of all Gathered Goods transported that excursion. This rate does not change based on the form Settlement takes.
>
> &nbsp;
>
> **3. FORM OF SETTLEMENT**
>
> 3.1 Settlement may be rendered in coin, in Gathered Goods, or in any combination thereof, **at the Bearer's choice.**
>
> 3.2 Where Settlement is rendered in Gathered Goods and no exact combination equals the sum owed, the Bearer shall surrender the smallest available combination that meets or exceeds it, and **the Concern shall return the difference in coin** at the time of Settlement. No excess is ever kept without a refund.
>
> 3.3 Where the Bearer has insufficient Gathered Goods or coin to fully satisfy Clause 2.1 on a given excursion, the shortfall carries forward as a debt against the Bearer's *next* excursion, interest-free, rather than being collected in a form the Bearer did not choose.
>
> &nbsp;
>
> **4. CHARGES**
>
> 4.1 A wagon maintenance fee of 1gp per excursion applies, stated here in full. No other charge of any kind attaches to this service.
>
> &nbsp;
>
> **5. VALUATION AND DISPUTE**
>
> 5.1 Either party may request the Tower's appraiser confirm an Assessed Value at any time, at no cost to either party.
>
> &nbsp;
>
> **6. SIGNATURE**
>
> *By boarding the wagon, the Bearer(s) affirm they have read and accept these Terms in full.*

**Mechanically:** this team now pays a genuine, verifiable 20% — coin, goods, their choice, change given, shortfalls carried forward instead of overcharged. It's not a reward on top of catching the trick; it's just what the original posted terms actually promised. Reiko doesn't extend it to any other team unless they independently earn it the same way — this is a one-team-at-a-time concession, not a floor-wide policy change.

## SELLING PHASE INTERRUPTIONS

Before resolving each customer slot, roll 1d20 modified by current Syndicate Pressure:

| Pressure | Interruption Chance |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | 1–2 on d20 (10%) |
| 4–6 | 1–3 on d20 (15%) |
| 7–9 | 1–4 on d20 (20%) |
| 10 | 1–6 on d20 (30%) |

On an interruption, roll 1d6:

| d6 | Result |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Tax Collector** — resolves against the current leading stall (or a Grudge-targeted team, if that check hits) |
| 2 | **Cutpurse Ring** *(Day 2+)* |
| 3 | **Slanderer** *(Day 3+)* |
| 4 | **Syndicate Stall price comparison** *(Day 3+, if open)* — no check, notes your best-seller; the Stall undercuts it tomorrow |
| 5 | **False alarm** — slot lost, nothing else happens |
| 6 | **DM's call** — whichever active threat fits the day |

## ENEMY: THE TAX COLLECTOR
20% of projected earnings demanded. **Pay** (lose 20%) · **Negotiate down** (Persuasion 14, pay 10%) · **Bluff** (Deception 14, delays 1d4 rounds) · **Refuse** (−1d6 customers, Pressure +1) · **Expose with evidence** (Persuasion 15, needs 1 clue, Rep +2, Pressure −1)

## ENEMY: THE CUTPURSE RING (Day 2+)
Ambient −1d4 customers all stalls. **Spot** (Perception 13) · **Drive off** (Intimidation 14, daily) · **Catch one** (Athletics 14, exposure) · **Hire crowd-watcher** (Persuasion 10, 3gp, negates 1 day)

## ENEMY: THE SLANDERER (Day 3+)
−1d6 customers + Rep −1 if uncountered. **Spot** (Perception 12) · **Counter in crowd** (Persuasion 13, negates) · **Counter-rumor** (Deception 12, reduces to −1d4) · **Expose publicly** (Persuasion 15, exposure, Rep +2)

## THE SYNDICATE STALL (Day 3, run by Doss)
−1d4 customers all stalls; −25% price on stocked categories. **Investigate supply** (Investigation 15, clue) · **Block supply** (Persuasion 16) · **Sabotage outside** (Stealth+Dex 14, Enforcers present) · **Expose with 2 clues** (Persuasion 16, Doss leaves permanently)

## ENEMY: THE ARSONIST (Days 4–5, Pressure 7+, a 50gp+ lead)
Cart loses 1d6 slots, −1d4 next phase. **Spot** (Passive Perception 14) · **Drive off** (Intimidation 14, Pressure −1) · **Defeat outside** (Combat, Pressure −2)

## Syndicate Enforcer (Combat Stats)
AC 13, HP 22, Speed 30ft, Shortsword +4 (1d6+2), Thrown dagger +4 (1d4+2, 20/60). Tax Collectors travel with 2.
