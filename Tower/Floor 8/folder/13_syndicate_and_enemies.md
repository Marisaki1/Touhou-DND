# FLOOR 8: THE MERCHANT'S CROSSING
### Part 13 — The Syndicate & Selling Phase Enemies

## Syndicate Pressure (town-wide, shared, 1–10, starts at 4)

| Level | State |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | Quiet |
| 4–6 | Active |
| 7–9 | Aggressive — Arsonist possible |
| 10 | Crisis — all enemy types active, Grey Ledger unlimited stock |

**Increases:** any stall leads by 20gp+ (+1) · agent driven off without exposure (+1) · Tax Collector ignored (+2) · Underground missed Day 3+ by choice (+1)
**Decreases:** agent publicly exposed (−1) · agent defeated outside bazaar (−2) · goods donated to residents (−1) · Charter found (−3, immediate) · a Storyline Evidence piece is turned in (−1, see `14`)

---

## Guaranteed Daily Syndicate Malice *(new)*

The Syndicate shouldn't feel like it only acts when a die happens to land right. From **Pressure 4 (Active) and above**, the Iron Ledger is doing *something* against the players every single day, whether or not the normal Selling Phase Interruption roll (below) ever triggers.

**At the end of any day where Pressure is 4+ and no Interruption fired during that day's Selling Phase**, roll once on this table — it's smaller than a full Interruption, but it guarantees the Syndicate never goes a full day without pushing back.

### Background Malice — d6

| d6 | Result |
|---|---|
| 1 | A rumor about "market irregularities" makes the rounds — no mechanical effect today, but Pressure narratively ticks toward the next Interruption (DM discretion, flavor only) |
| 2 | A supplier quietly declines to sell to the leading stall tomorrow morning until a Persuasion DC 12 smooths it over |
| 3 | One Common House NPC who was Friendly (`06`, result 8) becomes Wary instead — someone had a word with them |
| 4 | The Grey Ledger (Day 3+, if open) restocks early and undercuts one category by an extra 10% for the next Selling Phase |
| 5 | A Guard patrol is quietly redirected — Alertness gain from robbery is reduced by 1 for the next day only, but only because the Syndicate wants houses more exposed to a *different* threat (DM discretion — a rival team, a coming Interruption) |
| 6 | Nothing overt — but the DM should narrate a small unsettling detail (a black-tarnished coin left on a stall counter, a name scratched into a post) as a pure atmosphere beat |

### Grudge-Targeted Guaranteed Needle

Independent of the above, once a specific team's **Grudge Track** (below) reaches **3 or higher**, that team takes one guaranteed small setback per day even if no Interruption ever targets them directly:

| Grudge | Guaranteed Daily Effect |
|---|---|
| 3–5 (Targeted) | Once per day, DM narrates a minor needle — a rival gets a better delivery, a customer mentions "hearing something" about their stall. No hard mechanical penalty unless the team ignores it three days running, at which point apply a single −1 customer to their next Selling Phase. |
| 6–8 (Marked) | The continuous Slanderer campaign already in the Grudge table below applies daily without needing to roll it — −1d4 customers every day until countered. |
| 9–10 (Open Hostility) | The daily Tax Collector visit already specified in the Grudge table below applies without exception. |

## The Syndicate Grudge Track — per team, 0–10

The Syndicate doesn't treat every team equally. This tracks how much heat *your specific team* has drawn.

**Increases:** publicly exposing an agent dispatched at your stall +2 · leading all stalls by 20gp+ +1 · sabotaging/blocking the Grey Ledger +2 · finding the Iron Ledger Charter +3 · turning in a Storyline Evidence piece (`14`) +1 per piece, up to +3 total — the Syndicate knows exactly who's building a case against them

**Decreases:** a full day with no antagonism −1 · paying a Tax Collector's fee without resistance −1

| Grudge | Syndicate Behavior Toward This Team |
|---|---|
| 0–2 | Baseline — treated like anyone else in the shared interruption roll |
| 3–5 | Targeted — 50% chance any triggered interruption redirects to this team specifically, regardless of who's leading; see the Guaranteed Needle above |
| 6–8 | Marked — interruption rolls treat Pressure as +2 higher for this team only, and a continuous Slanderer campaign runs: −1d4 customers every day until countered |
| 9–10 | Open Hostility — a Tax Collector visits daily without exception; Reputation cannot rise above +3 while Grudge remains this high. Only fix: expose 2 more agents, or defeat Enforcers outside the bazaar. |

**Note:** a high Grudge Track is exactly what the hidden storyline in `14` is built around fighting back against — a team that's taken real heat from the Syndicate has, by definition, been gathering the evidence needed to end it.

## SELLING PHASE INTERRUPTIONS

Before resolving each customer slot, roll 1d20 modified by current Syndicate Pressure:

| Pressure | Interruption Chance |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | 1–2 on d20 (10%) |
| 4–6 | 1–3 on d20 (15%) |
| 7–9 | 1–4 on d20 (20%) |
| 10 | 1–6 on d20 (30%) |

On an interruption, roll 1d8:

| d8 | Result |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Tax Collector** — resolves against the current leading stall (or a Grudge-targeted team, if that check hits) |
| 2 | **Slanderer** *(Day 3+)* |
| 3 | **Cutpurse Ring** *(Day 2+)* |
| 4 | **Counterfeit Ring rumor** *(needs a 15gp+ sale to have occurred)* |
| 5 | **Mark-Hunter** *(Day 2+)* |
| 6 | **Grey Ledger price comparison** *(Day 3+, Grey Ledger open)* — no check, DM notes your best-seller; Grey Ledger undercuts it tomorrow |
| 7 | **False alarm** — slot lost, nothing else happens |
| 8 | **DM's call** — whichever active threat fits the day |

Reroll unavailable entries to 7.

## ENEMY: THE SYNDICATE TAX COLLECTOR
20% of projected earnings demanded. **Pay** (lose 20%) · **Negotiate down** (Persuasion 14, pay 10%) · **Bluff** (Deception 14, delays 1d4 rounds) · **Refuse** (−1d6 customers, Pressure +1) · **Expose with evidence** (Persuasion 15, needs 1 clue: exposure, Rep +2, Pressure −1) · **Report to Soren** (needs prior Investigate Syndicate) · **Confront outside** (Combat, 2 Enforcers, defeat = exposure)

## ENEMY: THE CUTPURSE RING (Day 2+)
Ambient −1d4 customers all stalls. **Spot** (Perception 13) · **Drive off** (Intimidation 14, daily) · **Catch one** (Athletics 14, exposure) · **Hire crowd-watcher** (Persuasion 10, 3gp, negates 1 day) · **Report to Soren**

## ENEMY: THE SLANDERER (Day 3+)
−1d6 customers + Rep −1 if uncountered. **Spot** (Perception 12) · **Counter in crowd** (Persuasion 13, negates) · **Counter-rumor** (Deception 12, reduces to −1d4) · **Expose publicly** (Persuasion 15, exposure, Rep +2) · **Confront outside** (Intimidation/Combat 14)

## ENEMY: THE COUNTERFEIT RING (Day 3+, after a 15gp+ sale)
−20% Persuasion price checks, all stalls, 1 day. **Locate alley** (Investigation 14) · **Expose fakes** (Persuasion 14, clears for your stall) · **Destroy stock outside** (Athletics/Combat 15, 2 days, exposure) · **Report via Soren** · **Undercut**

## ENEMY: THE GREY LEDGER STALL (Day 3, Edrin Voss)
−1d4 customers all stalls; −25% price on stocked categories. **Investigate supply** (Investigation 15, clue) · **Block supply** (Persuasion 16) · **Sabotage outside** (Stealth+Dex 14, Enforcers present) · **Report with 2 evidence** · **Expose with Charter** (Persuasion 16, 2 exposures, Voss leaves permanently) · **Undercut**

## ENEMY: THE ARSONIST (Days 4–5, Pressure 7+, a 50gp+ lead)
Cart loses 1d6 slots, −1d4 next phase. **Spot** (Passive Perception 14) · **Watch** (Perception 11) · **Drive off** (Intimidation 14, Pressure −1) · **Expose** (Athletics then Persuasion 14/15, Pressure −2) · **Defeat outside** (Combat, Pressure −2)

## ENEMY: THE MARK-HUNTER (Day 2+, via interruption roll #5)
**Ignore** (slot lost) · **Calm** (Persuasion 13) · **Expose** (Insight then Persuasion 12/13, exposure, Rep +1) · **Snap** (Intimidation 13; fail = Rep −1, −1d4 customers) · **Do nothing** (−1d4 customers)

## Syndicate Enforcer (Combat Stats)
AC 13, HP 22, Speed 30ft, Shortsword +4 (1d6+2), Thrown dagger +4 (1d4+2, 20/60), Ledger's Threat 1/combat (DC13 Wis save or Frightened). Tax Collectors travel with 2.

**Every exposure of an agent listed above is a Storyline Evidence source — see `14` for how these tie into bringing down the Iron Ledger for good.**
