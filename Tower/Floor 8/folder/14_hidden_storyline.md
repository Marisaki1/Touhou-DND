# FLOOR 8: THE HUMAN VILLAGE
### Part 14 — HIDDEN FLOOR BOSS: KAZUO

**DM-facing only.** Nothing on this page is shown to players directly — they discover it entirely through play. Kazuo is not part of the Syndicate, not part of the village, and not the reason this floor exists. He's passing through, running an errand for someone else — and that someone is deliberately left unnamed. This is a hook for a later floor, not a mystery this floor needs to solve.

---

## Who Kazuo Is

A quiet, unassuming man who is never quite where you expect him a second time. Crows follow him — not as a familiar or a pet, more like an entourage that answers to him. He speaks like a servant discussing someone else's business, never his own: *"I was asked to see that the two of you weren't left behind. Consider it evened out."* If pressed on who sent him: *"That's not something I'm able to share. Not yet. Maybe not on this floor at all."*

He is not hostile by default. He is not trying to sabotage the floor. He is running a small, specific errand — and the errand happens to be poisoned.

---

## The Jewelry

Three distinct pieces each time he appears — a jet-black hairpin, a tarnished silver bracelet, a clouded pearl necklace. All Masked White Girl-assessed at **~25gp base value each**. They are, mechanically, excellent trade goods: **Adult Woman NPCs (`06`) will always agree to buy a piece of this jewelry at full value with no check needed, and a Persuasion DC 12 push nets +20% instead of the usual +10%** — jewelry like this is simply a hit with them. Nothing about the item itself hints at the curse. It just sells exceptionally well.

**They are cursed.** Whoever ends up wearing/owning a piece long enough has years quietly drawn out of them. Nobody in the village knows this yet.

---

## The Appearances

### Day 2, morning

Kazuo appears — briefly, calmly — to the **two teams with the lowest cumulative earnings at the end of Day 1**. He hands each of those two teams **3 jewelry pieces** (one of each kind), says his one line about "evening things out," and is gone within a minute or two if not interrupted.

### Day 3, morning

Re-evaluate: Kazuo appears again to the **two teams with the lowest cumulative earnings at the end of Day 2** — not necessarily the same two teams as Day 2, if standings shifted. Same gift: **3 more jewelry pieces each**. Same line, roughly: *"Still evening it out. You're welcome."*

**If either appearance is met with hostility** — an attack, an attempt to grab him, an accusation shouted loud enough to draw a crowd — **he disappears immediately.** The crows scatter into a blinding flurry for a second or two; when it clears, he's gone. No fight happens here. This is not negotiable narratively — he is not meant to be caught this early, and DMs should protect this beat even if a player rolls exceptionally well on an attack or a grapple.

---

## Day 4, Morning: The Incident

**Whichever Adult Woman NPCs ended up owning a piece of the Day 2 jewelry batch — whether sold to them directly (`06`) or bought as a Special Customer at a team's stall (`12`) — age overnight into Old Woman NPCs.**

Mechanically: reclassify that NPC as an Old Woman for the rest of the floor (30gp cap instead of 60gp, if not already spent; her Info Table is now the Old Woman's table, not the Adult Woman's). Narratively: this is a genuine village-wide shock. Families are confused and frightened. Old Man/Old Woman and Adult Man/Adult Woman NPCs across the village start referencing it unprompted in their Info rolls (`06`, the Day 4+ rows).

**Tracing it back:** if the jewelry was sold openly at a team's stall during a Selling Phase, the connection is obvious within a day — no roll needed. If it was sold quietly via a private NPC Trade interaction, tracing it back to a specific team requires a Guard or Guild Investigation DC 15. A team that gets traced takes **Reputation −3** and a day of villagers being visibly wary of their stall (−1d4 customers, that day only) — a real consequence, not a campaign-ending one.

**DM tuning note:** if you want the Day 3 jewelry batch to carry the same consequence, the clean, symmetrical call is to have it manifest at the start of Day 5. The user's original request only specified the Day 4 incident for the Day 2 batch — treat the Day 5 echo as optional, and skip it if the floor's already resolving.

## Tracking Kazuo Down

Once the Day 4 incident has happened, players will likely want to find him. He can't just be stumbled into — he has to be tracked.

### Kazuo Clues (DM-tracked, per team, not shown as a number)

| Source | Yield |
|---|---|
| Investigating the Day 4 incident directly (Investigation DC 13, once it's public) | 1 clue |
| Any archetype's Day 4+ Info Table row referencing Kazuo (`06`) | 1 clue, per distinct NPC |
| Witnessing Kazuo unexpectedly and having him vanish on you | 1 clue (the sighting itself narrows down where he operates) |

### Two Ways In — Guild or Syndicate

- **The Guild route:** requires the team to have completed **3 or more Guild Quests** (`09`) — Quick Jobs don't count. A team that meets this can simply ask Scribe Hana for help (`17`); she's been quietly compiling crow sightings and hands over everything needed outright. (Only 3 Guild Quests refresh per day, split four ways — 3 completed across a five-day floor already means a team prioritized questing over other income, which is exactly the kind of investment this route should reward. Don't set this higher than 3 without also adding more daily quest postings. **At Trusted Guild Standing (8+, `09`), this drops to 2.**)
- **The Syndicate route:** requires the team's **Grudge Track at 2 or lower through Day 3** (`13`) *and* at least one instance of paying a Tax Collector's demand without resistance. A team on those terms can ask the Syndicate directly — they've noticed the crows too, and they don't like anything operating in their village that they don't get a cut of.

**Either route unlocks the Track Kazuo action outright, no further clues needed.** Without either route, a team needs **3 Kazuo Clues** gathered independently before they can attempt it.

### Track Kazuo (Major Action, 1 MAP, Investigation or Survival DC 15)

Following crow sightings and secondhand rumors to his actual hideout — a hollow deep in the Wildlife Forest, well off the marked gathering nodes.

- **Success:** the hideout is found. Proceed to `15`.
- **Failure:** no progress lost — the MAP is spent, but the team can try again another day. No penalty; he's elusive, not vindictive.

## Checking the Math — and a Real Risk to Watch For

Kazuo's gift is a real, meaningful boost for whichever teams are struggling most — 3 pieces of jewelry at ~25gp each, sold well, is worth roughly 75–90gp per gift. A team that stays in the bottom two both times (Day 2 and Day 3) can receive **both** gifts, worth up to ~150–180gp combined. Against a projected average team total of ~350–450gp over the floor (`07`), that's a **35–45% boost** for the two teams that get it — a real rubber-band, not a token gesture.

**The honest risk:** this is large enough that it can overshoot. A team that was merely unlucky on Day 1 (not genuinely playing worse) could receive both gifts, sell them well, and end up *ahead* of teams that played competently the whole floor without ever touching jewelry. That may be exactly the "even the tides" effect you want — but it's worth watching at the table rather than assuming it self-corrects.

**It's partially self-limiting**, which softens this some: jewelry sells into the same finite Adult Woman NPC gold caps every other team is drawing from (`06`, `07`) — a team cashing in cursed jewelry is partly just claiming NPC-trade gold that would otherwise have gone to whoever traded with those NPCs normally, not purely adding new gold to the floor. It's redistributive, not purely additive.

**If it's overshooting at your table:** the cleanest dial is dropping each gift from 3 jewelry pieces to 2 (cutting the swing to roughly ~50–60gp per gift, ~100–120gp for both) rather than changing the day triggers themselves, since the Day 2/Day 3 timing and "bottom two teams" targeting were specified directly and are worth keeping as written.

The cost side is real regardless of tuning: the jewelry is poisoned, and by the time anyone realizes it, the team that took the "free" help has already sold it to real, named villagers. What they do next — investigate, hide it, hunt Kazuo down, or just move on — is entirely theirs to decide.
