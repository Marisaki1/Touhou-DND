# FLOOR 8: THE MERCHANT'S CROSSING
### Part 14 — HIDDEN STORYLINE: "The Ledger's Debt"

**DM-facing only.** Nothing on this page is ever shown to players directly — they discover it entirely through play. This is the floor's secret spine: a way for one team to go past merely surviving the Syndicate's interference and actually gut it, walking away with a reward big enough to guarantee first place.

---

## The Secret

The Iron Ledger doesn't just tax the town — it's been quietly skimming from **itself**. Vael Geldrun's pact with the Hollow Ledger feeds on transactions, and the entity doesn't distinguish between town gold and Syndicate gold. For over a decade, a growing cut of every tax, fine, and "protection fee" collected by the Iron Ledger has been vanishing into a hidden vault beneath the Syndicate House — accessible only from the Underground, never from the guarded public entrance. Vael knows. He's let it happen, because the Hollow Ledger wants it fed, and he no longer has the will (or the self left) to stop it.

**275gp sits in that vault right now.** It is *not* town wealth — it was never going to reach a customer's hand, a Guild coffer, or a merchant's stall through any normal channel. That's why recovering it doesn't draw from the 3,000gp Town Economy Pool (`01`) and never triggers a recession tier. It's Syndicate money being taken back from the Syndicate.

---

## Discovery: Storyline Evidence

The storyline is gated behind collecting **Storyline Evidence** — a soft-tracked count the DM keeps privately, not shown to players as a number (narrate progress qualitatively: "you're starting to see a pattern" rather than "that's Evidence #4").

### Where Evidence comes from

| Source | Evidence Yield |
|---|---|
| Major Guild Quest: **The Informant's Gambit** (`09`) | 1 guaranteed piece |
| Major Guild Quest: **The Elder's Request** (`09`) | 1 guaranteed piece |
| **Old Man / Old Woman** Clue results referencing Vael, "Geldrun," or the Ledger's rise (`06`, result 6) | 1 piece, each distinct NPC, once |
| **Old Man / Old Woman** result 9 at a **Large house** ("household head... was once someone important") | 1 strong piece, approached respectfully (Persuasion/Insight DC 13) |
| **Investigate Syndicate** (`04`, Investigation DC 15) | 1-in-3 chance (roll d3, 1 hits) of yielding Evidence instead of/alongside the normal exposure clue |
| **Exposing** any Selling Phase enemy via their listed "Expose" option (`13`) | 1-in-2 chance (roll d2 or a coin flip) of an Evidence piece bonus, DM's call on flavor (a ledger page, a name, a symbol) |
| **Finding the Iron Ledger Charter** (`04`, Underground, Investigation DC 16) | 2 pieces — the single biggest source, and a hard prerequisite for Stage 2 below |
| Guild Hall quest **Iron Ledger Tip** (`09`) | 1 piece |

**Evidence belongs to whichever team collected it.** It is not shared town-wide. A team's storyline progress is tracked entirely on their own count.

## The Stages

### Stage 1 — The Pattern (3 Evidence)

Once a team has gathered **3 pieces**, the next time they bring their findings to a trusted NPC — the Large-house Old Man/Old Woman who gave them a piece is the natural choice, or failing that, a sympathetic Guild Hall contact — that NPC puts it together out loud: the numbers don't just show the Iron Ledger stealing from the town. They show gold *leaving the Syndicate's own books* and never coming back. Something inside the Iron Ledger is embezzling from the embezzlers.

This is a roleplay beat, not a check — but if you want a confirming roll, Investigation or Insight DC 14 lets the *players* be the ones to spot the pattern themselves, which usually lands better.

**Unlocks:** the ability to pursue Stage 2.

### Stage 2 — Decode the Vault Ledger (6 Evidence total, Charter required)

**New Major Action, Underground only:** *Decode the Embezzlement Ledger*
- **Rounds:** 6
- **Check:** Investigation DC 17
- **Requirement:** the Iron Ledger Charter must already have been found (`04`)
- **Success:** reveals the existence and rough location of a hidden vault beneath the Syndicate House, reachable only through an unmapped Underground passage — never through the Syndicate House's guarded public front.
- **Failure:** no progress lost, but the attempt costs the 6 rounds; failure by 5+ alerts a nearby Enforcer patrol (roll a Road Encounter-style check, `10`, treating it as a guaranteed "hit")

**Unlocks:** Stage 3.

### Stage 3 — Raid the Hollow Vault (one-time, floor-wide)

This plays like the Pristine Graveyard heist (`08`) in structure — a single, high-stakes, one-time prize that closes out for the whole floor once attempted successfully by anyone.

**Major Action:** *Raid the Hollow Vault*
- **Rounds:** 12
- **Check:** Stealth or Sleight of Hand DC 18
- **Guards:** 2 Syndicate Enforcers (`13` stat block) are stationed here — a team can attempt to bypass them entirely (contested against the same DC 18) or fight through, DM's call based on approach.

| Result | Outcome |
|---|---|
| **Success** | The team recovers the full **275gp Storyline Reward Reserve** — paid immediately, entirely outside the Town Economy Pool (`01`). Syndicate Pressure (`13`) drops by **3**, immediately. This team's Grudge Track resets to **0**. Reputation **+5**. The vault is empty now — remove it from play; no other team can attempt this stage again. |
| **Failure** | No gold. The 2 Enforcers engage in combat (`13` stat block, both). Syndicate Pressure **+2**, and this team's Grudge Track jumps to **6** minimum ("Marked") regardless of its prior value — the Iron Ledger now knows exactly who's been digging. |
| **Failure by 5+** | As above, plus a third Enforcer arrives as backup within 1d4 rounds. |

### Stage 4 — Aftermath and the Boss Fight

A team that completes Stage 3 successfully has done real, lasting damage to the Iron Ledger's financial backbone — and by extension, to what feeds Vael's pact with the Hollow Ledger. If this floor's Vael Geldrun encounter (`15`) is reached afterward:

- Vael's opening line changes to acknowledge it directly — he knows his own network was gutted from beneath him, and knows who did it.
- **Mechanical bonus for the team that completed the storyline:** Vael's Phase 1 passive (Market Manipulation) does not apply to this team for the first 3 rounds of combat — his hold on "the market" is visibly weaker around them.
- This is flavor-forward and meant to feel earned, not to trivialize the fight — HP and other stats are unchanged.

## Why 275gp Guarantees First Place

Cross-reference `01`: the Town Economy Pool is 3,000gp split across the *entire floor and all four teams*, dominated by Selling Phase income. The boss unlock condition in `15` requires only **150gp cumulative** earnings as its gold threshold — a reasonable benchmark for "a team that's playing competently but not dominating." A lump sum of 275gp, arriving on top of whatever a team has already earned through normal play, is large enough that only a truly exceptional selling run from a rival team could realistically overtake it. That's intentional: this storyline is meant to be *the* decisive move for a team willing to invest real time in investigation and Guild quests instead of pure sales grinding, without being so large that it trivializes the whole floor if found on Day 1 (it can't be — Evidence collection and the Charter prerequisite make Day 3 the earliest realistic completion, and that's before accounting for the Vault's own DC 18 and Enforcer guards).

**DM tuning knob:** if your table is playing looser/faster and Stage 3 is realistically reachable by Day 2, consider requiring the full 6-Evidence Stage 2 threshold to be raised to 8, or add a second required Major Quest completion, so it still lands as a Day 3–4 payoff rather than trivializing the earlier days.
