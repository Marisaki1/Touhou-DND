# FLOOR 8: THE MERCHANT'S CROSSING
### Part 16 — Rivals, Victory, & Rewards

## RIVAL STALL NPCs

### BRAM'S BOUNTY — Bram Hollander
Volume and breadth, general goods, thin margins. Day 1 bonus +5gp. Weak to a themed stall or a bought-out Supply Warehouse. Bribeable (Persuasion DC11) for a soft trend hint.

### THE IVORY COMPASS — Celeste Vane
Curated premium goods, rotating daily (d4: trinkets/imports/Excellent crafted/specimens). Thin supply, reputation-dependent (one rumor at Deception DC13 hurts her for a day). Alliance: sell her an 8gp+ unusual item, get +10% price for 1 day.

### IRONCLAD TRADES — Garrath Ironshod & sons
Practical durable goods. Day 1 bonus +3gp. Can't handle perishables, no performance ability. Alliance: good terms reduce Access Underground DC by 4.

## VICTORY CONDITIONS

**Floor Clears:** Day 5's selling phase ends and totals are calculated. **Minimum Clear:** any positive earnings above the starting seed by end of Day 5.

### Final Rankings

1st: highest total, 2 rare unidentified + 25% earnings bonus. 2nd: 1 uncommon unidentified + 10% bonus. 3rd: beat at least one rival, 1 consumable. Last (cleared): floor cleared, no bonus.

*(Reminder: the 275gp Storyline Reward Reserve from `14`, if recovered, counts toward a team's total for these rankings, but was never part of the 3,000gp Pool — so awarding it never risks the Pool going negative or upsetting the recession tiers in `01`.)*

### Boss Rewards (all players, if Vael is defeated)

1 rare unidentified each · +1 rare each if all 4 conditions met before Day 4 · 1 very rare to the player with lowest max HP if nobody dropped to 0

### Additional Bonuses (DM discretion)

Reputation +5+ by Day 5 → repeat customer follows to a future floor · all 6 Syndicate types exposed → 1 rare · Masterwork Product sold → 1 uncommon · Pressure reduced to 1 or lower → 1 rare · single-day 50gp+ → 1 uncommon · never lost a phase to a rival → 1 consumable · all 4 trends correctly ID'd in one day → 1 uncommon · **completed the full hidden storyline (`14`) → 1 rare unidentified, on top of the gold, representing a fragment of the Hollow Ledger's severed influence**
