# FLOOR 8: THE HUMAN VILLAGE
### Part 16 — Rivals, Victory, & Rewards

## RIVAL STALL NPCs

### BRAM'S BOUNTY — Bram Hollander
Volume and breadth, general goods, thin margins. Day 1 bonus +5gp. Weak to a themed stall or a bought-out Supply Warehouse. Bribeable (Persuasion DC 11) for a soft trend hint.

### THE IVORY COMPASS — Celeste Vane
Curated premium goods, rotating daily (d4: trinkets/imports/Excellent crafted/specimens). Thin supply, reputation-dependent (one rumor at Deception DC 13 hurts her for a day). Alliance: sell her an 8gp+ unusual item, get +10% price for 1 day.

### IRONCLAD TRADES — Garrath Ironshod & sons
Practical durable goods. Day 1 bonus +3gp. Can't handle perishables, no performance ability. Alliance: good terms reduce the Mining Cave's Syndicate encounter chance from 1-in-6 to 1-in-8 for your team for the rest of the floor.

## VICTORY CONDITIONS

**Floor Clears:** Day 5's Selling Phase ends and totals are calculated. **Minimum Clear:** any positive earnings above the starting seed by end of Day 5.

### Final Rankings

1st: highest total, 2 rare unidentified + 25% earnings bonus. 2nd: 1 uncommon unidentified + 10% bonus. 3rd: beat at least one rival, 1 consumable. Last (cleared): floor cleared, no bonus.

### Boss Rewards (the team that defeats Kazuo, `15`)

1 rare unidentified item · Reputation +4 · +2d8 bonus customers on Day 5 · Grudge Track −2

### Additional Bonuses (DM discretion)

Reputation +5 or higher by Day 5 → repeat customer follows to a future floor · all 5 Syndicate types exposed → 1 rare · Masterwork Product sold → 1 uncommon · Pressure reduced to 1 or lower → 1 rare · single-day 50gp+ → 1 uncommon · never lost a phase to a rival → 1 consumable · all 3 Market Trends correctly ID'd in one day → 1 uncommon · **tracked down and defeated Kazuo without ever selling a single piece of his jewelry → 1 rare, on top of the above, for a team that turned down easy money entirely**
