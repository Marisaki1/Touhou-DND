# FLOOR 8: THE HUMAN VILLAGE
### Part 17 — Named NPCs: Syndicate, Guards, Guild, and the Village Specials

Four rosters of 8, expanded to actually run at the table — each with a voice, a relationship to the people around them, a schedule, and (typically) two mechanical hooks rather than one. Use them as written, or as a base to reskin. (The three rival stall owners — Bram, Celeste, Garrath — round out the village's named cast; see `16`.)

---

## THE SYNDICATE — 8 Members

The Syndicate isn't a monolith. Reiko runs it like a business with employees who have their own opinions about the job — which is exactly the crack a team can work, if they're paying attention to who's talking to whom.

### 1. Reiko Kurogane — "The Broker"

Mid-forties, dressed sharper than anyone else in the village, deliberately — a small, constant reminder that she isn't one of them. Speaks in a level, unhurried voice and has never once needed to raise it to be frightening. Doesn't threaten; states consequences plainly, like weather. Widowed — her husband ran a legitimate trade house that the Syndicate broke years before she rose inside it, and whether she joined out of survival or genuine ambition is left deliberately unresolved; a DM can decide, or never say.

> *"I don't collect anything myself anymore. That's what makes people trust me with the numbers."*

**Schedule:** the Syndicate house, almost always. Occasionally makes an unannounced visit to whichever stall is leading.

**Relationships:** directs Doss, Jun, and Boruda directly. Suspects Sana's doubts but hasn't acted on them — whether out of genuine fondness or patience waiting for a better moment is unclear even to her. Privately respects Guildmaster Orin as the one person in the village playing a comparable long game.

**Hooks:**
- The gatekeeper for "Syndicate Friendly" standing and Kazuo's Syndicate tracking route (`14`) — meets teams only through a proven intermediary (Sana, or a track record of cooperation).
- Will personally negotiate a permanent flat 10% Tax Collector rate for the rest of the floor, in exchange for a team *not* publicly exposing an agent they've caught red-handed — a genuine moral trade-off, not a trap.
- If a team ever helps her with something adjacent to legitimate trade (a witnessed Negotiate Supply/Exclusive Deal gone well), she remembers — that team's Grudge Track decays twice as fast from then on.
- **Owns the Kurogane Carriage Concern** — the wagon service that runs teams out to the Gathering Areas and back (`13`, "Reiko's Wagon Concern"). The "20%" advertised on the booth is real, technically, and almost never what anyone actually pays. She'll grant a specific team the honest rate, permanently, if they beat her at Persuasion DC 16 rather than just complaining — she respects the ask more than the grievance.

### 2. Doss Varnick — Runs the Syndicate Stall

An outsider, foreign vowels, talks fast in the cadence of someone used to selling himself as much as his goods. Genuinely believes undercutting the village's stalls is just good business, not malice, and resents being the visible face of something he didn't design.

> *"It's not personal. Somebody's gotta run the numbers down, and I'm good at numbers."*

**Schedule:** the Syndicate Stall, Day 3 onward.

**Relationships:** reports to Reiko, mildly dislikes Boruda ("too much muscle, not enough math"), gets along with Nezumi — two outsiders in an insular operation.

**Hooks:**
- Exposing him with 2 clues (Persuasion DC 16, `13`) removes him permanently.
- Will trade a genuine Market Trend tip for being left alone one more day (Persuasion DC 13) — a bribe running the other direction.

### 3. "Iron-Purse" Jun — the Tax Collector

Thin, wire-spectacled, meticulous. Carries an actual small personal ledger — not a mystical one, just a man who checks his math. Not cruel, simply unmoved; he's heard every excuse already.

> *"Twenty percent. I've already done the math, so don't do it again out loud."*

**Schedule:** appears via the Selling Phase Interruption (`13`); occasionally seen "auditing" a house on the Whereabouts table (`05`).

**Relationships:** Sana was his understudy before being moved to street collection — he still checks in on her, oddly paternal for a tax man.

**Hooks:**
- Remembers being negotiated down: each successful "Negotiate down" against him this floor raises his DC by +1 the next time (`13`).
- Will knock 5% off a day's demand for a team that shows him proof of a completed Guild Quest — "professional courtesy," his phrase for it.

### 4. Nezumi — Cutpurse Ring Leader

Young, quick, quiet, calls everyone "boss" out of habit rather than deference. Doesn't hate anyone — steals because the alternative is being someone else's collection target instead.

> *"Wasn't here. Never was. Ask literally anyone."*

**Schedule:** ambient, roams Residential & Market and the Slums (`13`).

**Relationships:** something like friends with Doss; genuinely afraid of Boruda; quietly trades real information to Whistling Sae for a cut of the coin.

**Hooks:**
- If "Catch one" (`13`) specifically nets Nezumi: double the usual Grudge decrease, plus a free Kazuo Clue (`14`) — she hears everything on the street.
- If never caught, she'll eventually return one stolen item anonymously to whichever stall she's targeted least, once, Day 4 or later — professional courtesy in the other direction.

### 5. Otoha — the Slanderer

Theatrical, loves an audience, treats a whisper campaign like performance art. Not actually malicious so much as extremely good at this and a little too fond of being feared for it.

> *"I didn't say it happened. I said 'people are saying it happened.' Completely different."*

**Schedule:** wherever the campaign needs her, ambient (`13`).

**Relationships:** a professional rivalry with Whistling Sae — two information brokers with very different clientele and no love lost.

**Hooks:**
- Remembers losses: a team that's beaten her at Persuasion once gets +2 on all future Counter checks against her specifically.
- Can be flipped for a day — out-performing her at her own game (Performance DC 15) gets her spreading *good* rumors about that team instead, once.

### 6. Akai — the Arsonist

Burn-scarred hands, barely speaks. The Syndicate's break-glass option, and even Reiko is a little careful around him.

> *"...Don't need to be here long."*

**Schedule:** only Days 4–5, Pressure 7+ (`13`).

**Relationships:** takes orders from Reiko and no one else — a sign of how seriously fire is treated as an escalation, even by the Syndicate's own standards.

**Hooks:**
- Can be Spotted a day in advance — Passive Perception 14, the day *before* he strikes, for a team actively tracking Pressure.
- Standing orders to avoid any team with Reputation +5 or higher — a PR disaster the Syndicate doesn't want, which a savvy team can lean on defensively.

### 7. Boruda — Enforcer Captain

Big, calm, unfailingly polite even mid-shakedown — professional to a fault. Doesn't relish the work, doesn't resent it either.

> *"Nothing personal. Let's make this quick so we can both go back to our day."*

**Schedule:** leads Enforcer pairs/groups whenever the Tax Collector or a bigger threat needs muscle (`13`).

**Relationships:** mild disdain from Doss; oddly well-liked by some villagers (tips generously, never postures).

**Hooks:**
- Defeating him personally in combat drops Pressure by an extra 1, on top of the normal −2 — he's genuinely hard for the Syndicate to replace.
- Can be talked out of a specific encounter entirely (Intimidation or Persuasion DC 16, once per floor) — he has no real stake in dying for the job.

### 8. Sana — the One Having Doubts

Young, still visibly capable of being someone else. The Syndicate roster's soft spot, and the reason it isn't a monolith.

> *(privately, if trusted)* "I didn't grow up wanting to do this to people I know."

**Schedule:** junior collector duties, often paired with Jun.

**Relationships:** quietly still looked after by Jun; wary of Reiko noticing her hesitation; would leave the organization entirely given a real alternative.

**Hooks:**
- Insight DC 13 during any interaction reveals she'd talk if approached kindly and privately — a free, no-Evidence-needed alternate route into "Syndicate Friendly" standing for tracking Kazuo (`14`), bypassing the Grudge ≤2 requirement entirely.
- A team that goes further and offers her a genuine way out (a Guild Quest or connection resulting in real, legitimate work, DM's call) can trigger a small epilogue at floor's end — she leaves the Syndicate for good.

## THE GUARD — 8 Types

| 1 | Standard Patrol Guard | 15/26 (`11`) | The default, no personality required — whoever's on shift |

| 3 | Watchman Toro | 15/26 | Older, mournful about grave disturbances, tends them off-duty too |

### 2. Captain Housen

> *"You're not the first group to think the rules don't apply to them. You won't be the last to learn otherwise."*

Appears personally at Guard Investigation total 21+ (`11`). AC 17, HP 40; Guild Backup triggers automatically rather than on a roll.

**Hook:** has genuine authority to waive a fine once, for a team with real Guild Reputation, if approached respectfully *before* sentencing rather than after.

### 3. Watchman Toro

> *"Folks buried here didn't ask to be dug up twice — once by death, once by somebody's greed."*

The Graveyard Watch (`08`). Passive Perception 15 specifically for grave disturbances.

**Hook:** will warn a team off a specific graveyard attempt rather than catch them, if their Reputation is +2 or better — a mercy, not a rule.

### 4. Market Guard Ren

> *"Rule five is very clear, actually."*

Enforces the Violence Penalties (`01`) directly, responding within the same round to anything witnessed inside the market — no Patrol Check roll needed there.

**Hook:** easiest guard in the village to publicly embarrass — making him look foolish in front of a crowd (Performance or Deception DC 14) delays his enforcement response by a full round, once per day, out of sheer flustered hesitation.

### 5. Road Warden Kessa

> *"Bandits again. Wonderful. Truly, my favorite part of the week."*

Resolves Road Encounters (`10`). Mounted — arrives a round faster than a standard Patrol Check would suggest.

**Hook:** will escort a team's cart on request (Persuasion DC 12, once per floor), removing the Road Encounter roll entirely for that one trip.

### 6. Guild Hall Guard Aldric

> *"Let's not make this a whole thing. What actually happened?"*

Handles quest-related disputes at the Guild. Won't escalate to combat over anything short of real violence — the easiest guard in the village to de-escalate.

**Hook:** a positive relationship with him makes him a reliable, plain-spoken source for exactly how close a team is to the 3-Guild-Quest threshold for Kazuo's Guild route (`14`).

### 7. "Old Sourface" Berrin

> *"Nobody talks to the guard unless they want something. ...S'fine. Go on, what is it."*

The friendly off-duty guard from the Free Space table (`02`, seed 8).

**Hook:** trades patrol-schedule info for good conversation or a small bribe (Persuasion DC 10) — a legitimate way to learn Alertness/patrol timing before a robbery. If genuinely befriended (a real conversation, no check, just roleplay), he'll flag once when Alertness is about to hit a dangerous tier.

### 8. Investigator Mai

> *"I've been standing here the whole time. You didn't notice. That's sort of the point of my job."*

Plainclothes; handles the "a witness comes forward" branch of Guard Investigation (16–20 result, `11`). Insight +6 — very hard to bluff.

**Hook:** if a team genuinely beats her in a contested Insight check during an Investigation scene, she respects it enough to downgrade that result by one tier, once per floor.

## THE GUILD — 8 Members

The Guild is more than a quest board. It's the one civic institution in the village that isn't extracting from the players *or* extorting the village — see `09` for the new Guild Standing track, Bounty Board, Storage, and Arbitration systems this cast now plugs into.

### 1. Guildmaster Orin

> *"The board doesn't lie, but it doesn't tell the whole truth either. Ask more than it wants you to."*

Distant, dignified, cares about the village more than the Guild's procedure, which occasionally puts them at odds.

**Schedule:** rarely seen directly — appears in person for a Major Quest completion, or at Guild Standing "Ally" tier (`09`).

**Hook:** can unilaterally override the "3+ Guild Quests" threshold for Kazuo's Guild route (`14`), for a team that's done something genuinely heroic, DM's discretion.

### 2. Quartermaster Sable

> *"Posted is posted. First claim, fair claim. I don't play favorites and I don't apologize for it."*

Brisk, fair, treats every team identically — refreshing, after the Syndicate.

**Schedule:** manages day-to-day quest postings and payouts (`09`).

**Hook:** will tell a team, honestly and exactly, how many Guild Quests they've banked toward Scribe Hana's threshold, if asked directly.

### 3. Master Smith Tetsu

> *"You had a perfectly good piece of iron ore and you made THAT?"*

Gruff, proud, genuinely skilled, mildly wounded by amateurs wasting good materials.

**Schedule:** the Guild Hall table (`09`, result 1).

**Hook:** will craft a team's *first* Product of the floor personally, for free, once, if asked politely and the materials are provided.

### 4. Apprentice Mikk

> *"I meant to do that. I definitely meant to do that."*

Clumsy, eager, wants to be Tetsu's protégé, over-explains everything out of nerves.

**Schedule:** the Guild Hall table (`09`, result 2).

**Hook:** if befriended, he becomes a recurring bit — occasionally "helps" with a Craft check (disadvantage), but reliably passes along whatever Guild gossip he's overheard, unprompted, once trust is built.

### 5. Scribe Hana

> *"I don't gossip. I keep records. There's a difference, even if the records happen to be about gossip."*

Precise, quiet, unnervingly well-informed — the smartest person in the Guild, and everyone quietly knows it.

**Schedule:** the Guild's records room.

**Hook:** the named contact behind Kazuo's Guild tracking route (`14`) — she's been compiling crow sightings. Will also trade a rival team's approximate standing for a genuine piece of information in return, once.

### 6. Combat Instructor Dagen

> *"Rule one of surviving a bandit fight: don't be the slowest one running. Rule two: there is no rule two, just don't be slow."*

Weary veteran energy, dry humor about past near-misses, a genuinely good teacher.

**Schedule:** briefs teams taking combat-flavored Guild Quests (`09`).

**Hook:** a pre-quest conversation grants Advantage on the first initiative roll of that quest. Sparring with him for a round (no risk) grants Advantage on the next Athletics or Intimidation check that day, player's choice.

### 7. Bursar Renna

> *"That's three copper short. I will find the three copper. I will find it if it takes all week."*

Exacting about money to a comedic degree, otherwise perfectly pleasant, visibly good at her actual job.

**Schedule:** manages the Guild's coffers and quest payouts.

**Hook:** deliberately shortchanging her costs Reputation −1 with the Guild specifically. Consistently exact (or generous) payment earns a standing 5% bonus on all future Guild Quest payouts for that team.

### 8. Groundskeeper Pell

> *"Folks don't look twice at a man with a rake. Funny, what you notice when nobody's looking at you."*

Older, quiet, tends the grounds like they matter, easy to overlook, sees everything.

**Schedule:** the Guild Hall grounds; lost his tool (`09`, Quick Job #3).

**Hook:** unprompted return of his tool earns a standing favor — once per floor, he'll vouch for a team to Scribe Hana even below the 3-quest threshold. A friendly chat with him (no check) reveals one Free Space's contents or one house's current Whereabouts roll, once per day.

## VILLAGE SPECIALS — 8 Unique NPCs

One-of-a-kind fixtures, no archetype table (`06`) needed.

### 1. Shrine-Keeper Aoi

> *"Everyone ends up here eventually. I try to make sure that's not the worst part of their story."*

Serene, gently sad, devoted to the dead being respected regardless of who they were in life. Tends both graveyards (`08`).

**Hooks:** a Religion DC 10 conversation grants Advantage on the next Salvage the Public Graveyard check, once per day. Tell her the truth about a Pristine Graveyard attempt rather than lying, and she won't report it — a real, testable trust mechanic.

### 2. "Crooked" Emil — the Fence

> *"Crooked's just a business model, friend. I never once shorted a customer. Ask around."*

Wry, unbothered by his reputation, genuinely fair within his own crooked rules (`10`).

**Hooks:** sells 5+ items to him this floor and his cut improves permanently from 60–70% to a flat 75% — loyalty pricing. Occasionally has a genuinely rare item for sale himself, sourced from "elsewhere" — the only place in the village to buy something off no Gathering table at all.

### 3. Auntie Ren — the Soup Kitchen Cook

> *"Sit. Eat. We'll talk about what you did or didn't do after you've eaten."*

Warm, tired, generous despite having little herself (`10`).

**Hooks:** two Soup Kitchen donations, and the third earns a free Small item "for the road," no action cost. A team known to her gets a friendlier default Info result from any Young/Teen NPC asked about them.

### 4. Grath the Bonebreaker — the Fight Ring Master

> *"Ring's got rules. Rules keep it fun instead of ugly. I like fun better."*

Huge, scarred, far gentler than his name — genuinely retired from real violence (`10`).

**Hooks:** will occasionally throw a match for a team he's taken a liking to, quietly, never announced. Will train a PC between matches — Advantage on their next Athletics check that day.

### 5. Whistling Sae — Rumor Alley's Broker

> *"Everybody thinks I want coin. I want to KNOW things. The coin's just how I keep the lights on."*

Restless, always moving, loves information for its own sake (`10`).

**Hooks:** trade her a real rumor for a real rumor and skip the RAP cost entirely. Bring her anything about Kazuo specifically and she gets unusually serious — gives it back doubled. She's heard the crow rumors too.

### 6. Wainwright Boro — Cart & Wagon Smith

> *"Anybody can nail some boards together. I build things that last."*

Practical, cheerful, takes real pride in the work. The one who actually builds cart upgrades (`01`).

**Hooks:** present for any Reinforced Cart or Merchant's Wagon attempt — one prior friendly interaction removes disadvantage on the build check even without formally Borrowing a Tool. Will reserve a set of upgrade materials a day ahead (Persuasion DC 10), guaranteeing availability instead of leaving it to chance.

### 7. Elder Kestrel — the Village Historian

> *"I've buried three generations of this village's gossip. Ask me something worth remembering."*

Ancient, sharp-eyed, the village's actual memory. A singular NPC, not an archetype.

**Hooks:** any Info request to her is an automatic success on the *best* row of the Old Woman table (`06`), no roll needed, once per floor. At Reputation +5 and a real conversation (no roll), she has an actual half-memory of "a story about servants who trade in years" predating Kazuo's arrival — a rare, deliberately vague breadcrumb toward his unnamed master, left open for a future floor.

### 8. Nana Yew — Midwife and Healer

> *"I'm not going to tell you it doesn't hurt. I'm going to make sure it stops."*

Brisk, no-nonsense, kind in a practical rather than sentimental way.

**Hooks:** basic healing/rest services for a modest fee or a Small item. If the Day 4 Kazuo incident happens, she's the one actually caring for the aged women afterward — approached with genuine concern rather than curiosity, she becomes the single richest source of Kazuo Clues in the village during Days 4–5 (`14`).
