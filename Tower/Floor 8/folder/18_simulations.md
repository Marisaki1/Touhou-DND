# FLOOR 8: THE HUMAN VILLAGE
### Part 18 — A Worked Simulation: Five Days, One Team

**Rebuilt from scratch.** The previous version of this file was built on a Production Phase that only worked out to 2.5 real hours — wrong. This version runs on the actual clock: **8:00 AM to 6:00 PM (10 hours, 10 MAP) for the Production Phase, 6:00 PM to 10:00 PM (4 hours) for Market Time**, exactly as `01` now specifies. Every clock time below is real and adds up.

**The team — Team Southwest:**
- **Kesa Vell**, human ranger — Survival/Stealth, gathering and robbery
- **Priya Osei**, half-elf bard — Persuasion/Performance, NPC talk, selling
- **Dorn Emberhand**, dwarf artificer — crafting and Athletics

**Reminder baked into every day:** per Rule 10 (`01`), this team never splits across districts — every Major Action below is the whole team, together, in one place. Round Actions (individual NPC chats, stall prep) vary per character, but only while the team is stationed somewhere as a group.

## DAY 1

**Morning:** Alertness 0. Market Trend: Hunt **Liked (+30%)**, Mine Neutral, Fish Neutral, Forage Disliked (−25%). Guild board refreshes.

**8:00 AM.** At the stall. Priya spends **1 RAP** talking to a nearby Adult Man (`06`) — a Market Trend hint (confirms Hunt is trending), then asks him outright whether anyone can turn raw materials into something better. Guaranteed answer, no roll (`09`): *"Guild Hall'll do it, if you've got the pieces for it."* **This is the moment Team Southwest learns crafting exists** — nobody volunteered it; Priya just asked. Dorn spends 2 RAP price-marking the empty cart. Kesa spends 2 RAP casing a nearby house for later. **~25 minutes used; it's 8:25 AM.**

**8:25–9:00 AM.** They head for the Wildlife Forest (1 hop, 2 RAP / 10 minutes), arriving with a few minutes to spare before the window opens.

**9:00 AM – 12:00 PM — Gather: Hunt (Major Action, 3 MAP).** This runs the full 3 hours, landing exactly at the window's own close. Kesa leads Survival:
- Node 1 (Medium, DC 13): rolls 14 — success. **Wild fowl, 6gp base.**
- Node 2 (Large, DC 16): rolls 19 — success. **Boar, 12gp base.**

**12:00 PM.** Travel back (2 RAP), arriving ~12:10 PM.

**12:10 PM – 1:10 PM — Rob a House, Small (Major Action, 1 MAP).** Houses are right there in Residential & Market — no extra travel. Kesa: Stealth DC 12, rolls 13 (a normal success, beating the DC by only 1 — Alertness +1, `05`). **1 Small item (herb, 2gp) + 1d4gp cash (rolled 3gp).**

**1:10–1:40 PM.** Downtime at the stall (RAP): Priya chats up another NPC (Info roll, flavor), Dorn preps the cart, Kesa cases a second house for tomorrow.

**1:40–2:00 PM.** Travel to the Guild (2 RAP), take the always-posted Quick Job — *Sweep the Guild Hall Steps* (2 RAP, no check, 3gp + Reputation +1) — Dorn handles it while Priya chats with **Groundskeeper Pell** (`17`) and Kesa glances at the day's three Guild Quests without committing yet.

**2:00–3:00 PM — Investigate Market Trend (Major Action, 1 MAP).** Confirms Hunt's trend for certain, useful for tonight's pricing.

**3:00–6:00 PM.** Final prep: sign posted (+1d4 customers), display adjusted (+5%), cart organized. **Total MAP spent today: 5 of 10** (3 Gather + 1 Rob + 1 Investigate) — a measured day, not a maxed one.

**Market Time, 6:00–10:00 PM:** base customers 1d4+3, rolled **5**, +3 from the sign.
- Farm laborer buys the herb: 6sp.
- Young couple buys the wild fowl (Hunt trend + a made Upsell, DC 12): **9gp.**
- Passing merchant buys the boar (trend + Storytelling, DC 12 made): **17gp.**

**Day 1 total: 26gp 6sp + the 3gp robbery cash already in hand = ~30gp.** When totals come in, Team Southwest and Team Northeast are tied for lowest.

## DAY 2 — Kazuo's First Visit

**8:00 AM.** Because they finished lowest, **Kazuo is waiting near the stall** (`14`) — crows on the awning, three pieces of jewelry in hand (hairpin, bracelet, necklace, ~25gp each). *"I was asked to see that the two of you weren't left behind. Consider it evened out."* Gone within the minute. The team argues briefly, keeps the jewelry.

**8:00–10:00 AM — Gather: Fish (Major Action, 3 MAP), Window B (10–1) technically starts at 10, so they walk to the River Shallows first (2 RAP) and use the early minutes on RAP prep, starting the Gather right at 10:00.** Dorn leads with Nature (he wants River Shallows ore-adjacent... actually he leads Sleight of Hand for the catch): Medium node (DC 13, rolls 15): **River trout, 6gp.** Large node (DC 16, rolls 12 — fails, no penalty, node stays available).

**1:00–1:20 PM.** Travel back (2 RAP). At a Free Space along the way, Priya spots an Adult Woman and sells her the hairpin outright — Adult Women auto-accept jewelry at full value, no check (`14`): **25gp**, 2 RAP.

**1:20–1:40 PM.** A second Adult Woman near the fountain — a regular the team's started calling **Mira** — buys the bracelet the same way: **25gp**, 2 RAP.

**1:40–4:40 PM — Resolve a Guild Quest: Cat Herder (Accept 1 RAP, travel 2 RAP, objective 2 MAP).** A Young Girl at a Free Space points them at two of the three cats for free; the third takes actual legwork. Success: **1d4+3gp** (rolled 6gp) + Reputation +1.

**4:40–6:00 PM.** Prep, holding the necklace back deliberately for a Special Customer.

**Market Time, 6:00–10:00 PM:** 1d6+3 customers, rolled **7**. On the last slot, d20 rolls **20 — Special Customer**, then d8 rolls **1, a Collector** — the necklace matches his taste. **3× base value: 75gp.** Ordinary sales of the day's trout add another 8gp.

**Day 2 total: 25 + 25 + 6 + 75 + 8 = 139gp.** Team Southwest jumps from last place to a comfortable middle rank.

## DAY 3 — No Visit This Time

**8:00 AM.** Kazuo re-evaluates: Team Southwest climbed out of the bottom two, so **he doesn't appear to them today** — he's visiting whoever's lowest now instead.

**8:00–9:00 AM.** Downtime/prep while waiting for a window.

**9:00 AM – 12:00 PM — Gather: Mine (Major Action, 3 MAP, Window D doesn't open till 2 — they use Window A's overlap... correction: Mine is Window B (10–1) or D (2–5). They wait until 10:00 to start properly, using 8–10 AM for prep and a Guild check-in instead.** Starting at 10:00 AM: Medium node (DC 13, rolls 17): **Copper ore, 6gp.**

**1:00–2:00 PM — Craft a Medium Product (Major Action, 2 MAP).** Dorn now has 4 Medium Mine items banked across two days. Tool Proficiency DC 14, rolls 17 — success. **1 Medium Mining Product, ~34gp** (the +50% markup from `03`) — meaningfully more than the raw copper's separate value.

**2:00–5:00 PM — Rob a House, Large (Major Action, 3 MAP).** Feeling flush, and using yesterday's banked house intel: Stealth DC 16, rolls 21 — a **clean success** (beat the DC by 5+, only +4 Alertness instead of +5, `05`). **1 Large item (iron ore, 10gp) + 3d6gp cash (rolled 12gp).**

**5:00–6:00 PM.** Prep. Kesa asks an Old Man for information (1 RAP) — a Syndicate Tip, filed away.

**Market Time, 6:00–10:00 PM:** 1d6+3, rolled 6. Before the second customer, an Interruption fires — **"Iron-Purse" Jun** (`17`), the Tax Collector, wants 20%. Priya negotiates down (Persuasion DC 14, rolls 16): **pays 10% instead.** The Medium Product sells for 30gp, the iron ore for 9gp.

**Day 3 total:** 12gp (robbery cash) + 30gp (Product) + 9gp (ore) = 51gp, minus Jun's 10% cut off the top ≈ **46gp.**

## DAY 4 — The Incident

**8:00 AM.** The village is talking about one thing. **Mira** — the Adult Woman who bought Team Southwest's bracelet two days ago — went to sleep looking the way she always has and woke up an old woman overnight. **Nana Yew** (`17`) is with her. Dorn puts it together first: *"We sold her something two days ago. We don't know what that hairpin and bracelet actually were."*

**8:00–9:00 AM — Investigate the incident (Major Action, 1 MAP, Investigation DC 13, now that it's public).** Rolls 15 — success. **1 Kazuo Clue** (`14`).

**9:00–10:00 AM — Talk to Elder Kestrel (1 RAP for the conversation itself, but she takes it seriously and it runs long — call it folded into the hour with other prep).** A real conversation, not a transaction. Her Info Table auto-succeeds on its best row for a team she takes seriously: *"There's an old story about servants who trade in years instead of coin. I didn't think it was real."* **A second Kazuo Clue.**

**10:00 AM – 12:00 PM — Resolve a Guild Quest: Poacher's Trail (Accept 1 RAP, travel 2 RAP, objective 1 MAP).** Success: 18gp + a Syndicate clue. **This is the team's 3rd Guild Quest completed across the floor** — enough for the Guild route to Kazuo (`14`).

**12:00–6:00 PM.** Subdued prep — the team isn't in the mood to push hard today.

**Market Time, 6:00–10:00 PM:** 1d6+4, rolled 8, but a quieter evening.

**Day 4 total: 18gp + modest ordinary sales ≈ 44gp.**

## DAY 5 — Finding Kazuo

**8:00 AM.** With 3 Guild Quests done, straight to **Scribe Hana** (`17`) instead of the board. She's been compiling crow sightings for exactly this: *"Wildlife Forest. Off the marked paths. I'd go before midday, if I were you."*

**8:00–9:00 AM — Track Kazuo (Major Action, 1 MAP, Investigation or Survival DC 15).** Kesa leads. Rolls 17 — success. The hideout is found.

**9:00–9:20 AM.** Travel there (a longer approach than a normal Gathering trip, given how far off the paths it is — the DM rules a slightly extended 20-minute hike, 4 RAP).

**The fight (`15`):** Kazuo doesn't run — there's nowhere left to vanish to. Two phases; the team takes real damage in Phase 2's Last Flock burst, but brings him down before a second Blinding Flock recharges. **Victory.** Reputation +4, Syndicate Grudge −2, one unidentified black feather that never quite lands.

**Late morning–6:00 PM.** Return, restock, final prep.

**Market Time, 6:00–10:00 PM:** base 1d8+4, rolled 9, **+2d8 for defeating Kazuo** (rolled 11) — **20 customers**, the biggest night of the floor. A Wealthy Eccentric special customer alone accounts for a large chunk of it.

**Day 5 total: 148gp.**

## What This Run Demonstrates

- **The day is a real 10-hour clock (8:00 AM – 6:00 PM), not an abstract pool.** Every Major Action above has an actual start and end time, and Gather actions land naturally against the four fixed windows from `03`.
- **MAP and RAP are genuinely different denominations of the same clock** — a team can see exactly how much of their day a choice costs, in hours or in 5-minute chunks, and plan around it.
- **The team never split up**, per Rule 10 — every Major Action involved all three characters in the same place at the same time.
- **Crafting stayed a secret until someone thought to ask** — Day 1, Priya asked an Adult Man directly, and that's the only reason the team ever learned the Guild Hall could do it.
- **Robbery paid more per MAP than gathering, and it was still riskier** — Team Southwest only attempted 2 robberies across 5 days, leaning on gathering as their steady, renewable income and using robbery opportunistically when the risk felt worth it.
- **Kazuo's rubber-band responded to the table's actual state** — he showed up because they were losing, and stopped the moment they weren't.
